Creating a ChimeEdu account is free and takes less than two minutes. Once you're signed up, you can join your teacher's classroom, complete activities, and track your progress across all your subjects — from any device.

## Steps to create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to app.chimeedu.com/register">
    Open [app.chimeedu.com/register](https://app.chimeedu.com/register) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your name and email">
    Enter your **first name**, **last name**, and **email address**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your role">
    In the **I am a…** dropdown, select **Student**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your school (optional)">
    If your teacher has asked you to, enter your school or institution name. This helps your teacher identify you when you join their classroom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your username">
    ChimeEdu will auto-generate a username for you. You can keep it or customise it to something you prefer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a strong password">
    Enter a password that is **at least 10 characters** long and includes an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number, and a symbol. Confirm it in the next field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the Terms and Privacy Policy">
    Read and tick the checkbox to agree to ChimeEdu's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Account">
    Select **Create Account** to submit your registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email for a confirmation link">
    ChimeEdu will send a confirmation email to the address you used. Open it and click the confirmation link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the link to confirm and sign in">
    After confirming, you'll be signed in and taken to your ChimeEdu dashboard. You're ready to join a classroom.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Use your school email address if your teacher has asked you to — it helps them identify you quickly when you join their classroom.
</Note>

## Signing in

Once your account is confirmed, sign in at [app.chimeedu.com/login](https://app.chimeedu.com/login):

- Enter your **email** and **password**, then click **Sign In**.
- If you've set up a **Passkey** (biometric login), you can use **Sign in with Passkey** instead.

## Forgot your password?

If you can't remember your password:

1. Go to [app.chimeedu.com/forgot-password](https://app.chimeedu.com/forgot-password).
2. Enter the email address linked to your account.
3. Check your inbox for a password reset email and click the link inside.
4. Set a new password and sign in.

<Tip>
  Save your login details somewhere safe — your email, username, and password. You'll need them every time you sign in.
</Tip>
