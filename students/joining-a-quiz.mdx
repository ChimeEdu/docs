Your teacher runs a live quiz from their screen; you play along on your own phone, tablet, or laptop. No app, no account, no setup.

## Joining

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the Quiz ID">
    Your teacher will show a **6-character Quiz ID** on the board — something like `GS7OB6`. There may also be a QR code you can scan instead.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to the join page">
    Open [app.chimeedu.com/join](https://app.chimeedu.com/join) in any browser and enter the Quiz ID. Capital letters don't matter — `gs7ob6` works just as well.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter a display name">
    Type the name your teacher will recognize on the leaderboard. If you'd rather not use your own name, choose the option to join with a randomly generated nickname.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for the start">
    You'll see **"Waiting for Quiz to Start"** with the name you joined as. Your teacher starts the quiz when everyone's in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Careful with characters that look alike: `0` and `O`, `1` and `I`. If the code is rejected, try the other one.
</Tip>

## Playing

When a question appears, tap your answer and submit. A few things worth knowing:

* **Answer options are numbered** — 1, 2, 3, 4 — not lettered.
* **Some questions have more than one correct answer.** If you see tick boxes, select every option you think is right before submitting.
* **You get one attempt per question.** Once you submit, your answer is locked.
* **You won't find out straight away.** After submitting you'll see *"Answer submitted! Waiting for results..."* — right and wrong are only revealed when your teacher reveals them for the whole class.

## Seeing how you did

When your teacher reveals the results, your device shows whether **you** got that question right, along with the correct answer.

<Note>
  The leaderboard lives on your teacher's screen, not yours — look up at the board to see the class standings. Your device shows your own result for each question, not the rankings.
</Note>

## If you get disconnected

If your connection drops mid-quiz, you'll leave the game and your score won't be waiting for you. You can rejoin with the same Quiz ID, but you'll come back in with a clean slate, so tell your teacher what happened.

If your **teacher's** connection drops, don't close anything — you'll see a message saying the quiz is paused while they reconnect. It picks up automatically, usually within a few minutes.

## Frequently asked

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a ChimeEdu account?">
    No. Live quizzes are open to anyone with the Quiz ID. You only need an account for classrooms, assessments, and saving flash card progress.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my quiz score saved to my account?">
    No. Live quiz scores aren't stored anywhere — not for guests, and not if you're signed in. They exist during the session and are gone once it ends. Signing in first doesn't change that.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The code isn't working.">
    Three common reasons: the quiz hasn't started or has already ended, the code was mistyped (check `0`/`O` and `1`/`I`), or it was a temporary quiz that has expired — those last 8 hours. Some quizzes also ask for a password, which your teacher will give you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use my phone?">
    Yes — any modern mobile browser works. Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge are all fine.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
