Joining a classroom puts everything your teacher has published — quizzes, assessments, flash cards, maps, timelines, study guides, and Experiences — on your dashboard in one place.

## What you need

Your teacher's **classroom code**: 8 characters, letters and numbers, like `KJ4M7NRW`. They might give it to you directly, project a QR code, or send you a link with the code already in it.

<Note>
  This is not the same as a **Quiz ID**. Quiz IDs are 6 characters and get you into a live quiz without an account. A classroom code is 8 characters and requires an account.
</Note>

## If you don't have an account yet

Follow your teacher's sign-up link, or go to [app.chimeedu.com/register](https://app.chimeedu.com/register) and enter the classroom code there. You'll be enrolled in the classroom as part of creating your account — see [Create your student account](/students/create-account) for the full walkthrough.

## If you already have an account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Log in at [app.chimeedu.com/login](https://app.chimeedu.com/login) with your email address and password.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the Join form">
    On your student dashboard, look for the option to join a classroom.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the code">
    Type your teacher's 8-character classroom code and submit. You're enrolled straight away — there's nothing to wait for and no approval needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you already have an account, clicking your teacher's sign-up link **won't** enroll you — that link only works while creating a new account. Sign in and enter the code on your dashboard instead.
</Warning>

## After you join

Open the classroom to see everything your teacher has published, sorted with the newest first. You can switch between tabs for activities, assessments, study guides, and Experiences, and search or re-sort the list.

Some items need a little more before you can open them — assessments always ask for a password, and some study guides and Experiences do too. Your teacher will give you those.

## Frequently asked

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I join more than one classroom?">
    Yes — as many as you need. Enter each classroom's code from your dashboard; you don't need a separate account for each.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My code isn't working.">
    Check you've typed all 8 characters correctly and that you're not using a 6-character Quiz ID by mistake. Teachers can also regenerate a classroom code, which retires the old one — ask your teacher for the current code.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I joined but the classroom looks empty.">
    Your teacher may not have published anything yet, or may have published items that are listed elsewhere. Give it a moment, refresh, and check with your teacher.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I leave a classroom?">
    Ask your teacher — they manage the student list for their classroom.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
