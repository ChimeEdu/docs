Once you're enrolled in a classroom, every activity your teacher publishes will appear in your classroom feed — no extra links needed. Quizzes, assessments, study guides, flash card decks, maps, timelines, and journal prompts all show up in one place, in the order your teacher sets.

## Steps to join a classroom

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the classroom join link from your teacher">
    Your teacher will share a unique join link with you. They may post it in class, send it by email, or share it through your school's learning management system (LMS).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the link">
    Open the join link in your browser. It will take you directly to the classroom enrolment page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to your ChimeEdu account">
    Sign in with your email and password. If you don't have an account yet, select **Sign up for free** and [create one](/students/create-account) — it only takes a couple of minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're enrolled">
    Once signed in, you're automatically enrolled in the classroom. It will appear on your ChimeEdu dashboard, ready to open.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your classroom feed

Once you're enrolled, your classroom feed is where everything happens. It shows:

- All **activities** your teacher has published — quizzes, flash cards, maps, timelines, and fill-in-the-blank exercises.
- **Assessments** you need to complete, with due dates if your teacher has set them.
- **Study guides** to read and reference while you revise.
- **Experience journals** for any field trips or real-world activities your class is documenting.

Everything appears in the order your teacher has set, so you always know what's current.

## What if you don't have a join link?

Ask your teacher — they're the only one who can share it. Your teacher generates the classroom join link from their dashboard and can resend it at any time.

<Note>
  Each classroom has its own unique join link. If you study multiple subjects on ChimeEdu, you may have a different join link for each one — and you can be enrolled in several classrooms at once.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Bookmark your classroom page after you join so you can open it quickly before every lesson — no need to go through the dashboard each time.
</Tip>
