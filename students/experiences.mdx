Experiences are structured field trips or collaborative journeys. Your teacher sets up the locations and prompts in advance — you check in, write journal entries, and document what you observe along the way. Everything you capture becomes part of a living record of the experience that your teacher can build on back in the classroom.

## Participating in an Experience

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Experience from your classroom feed">
    Find the Experience activity in your classroom dashboard and tap or click to open it. You'll see the travel map and the list of planned locations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the travel map and planned locations">
    The map shows the route your teacher has plotted and all the locations where you'll be stopping. Tap a location to preview what's planned there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check in at each location">
    When you arrive at a location, use the **Check In** button on your device. This logs your arrival and unlocks the prompt for that stop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read your teacher's prompt">
    Each location has a prompt written by your teacher. Read it carefully — it tells you what to observe, think about, or reflect on before you write.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your journal entry">
    Use the journal editor to write your response. Add text, attach photos from your device, and include any observations the prompt asks for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your entry">
    When your entry is complete, tap **Submit**. Your entry is sent to your teacher for review and added to the Experience log.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Take photos as you go and attach them to your entries — they make your journal much richer and help you remember details when you're back in the classroom.
</Tip>

## Writing journal entries

The journal editor gives you the tools to create detailed, expressive entries:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Rich text" icon="bold">
    Format your writing with bold, italics, headings, and bullet points to organise your thoughts clearly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Add photos" icon="image">
    Attach photos directly from your device's camera roll or take a new one on the spot to capture what you're seeing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Structured prompts" icon="list">
    Your teacher's prompts guide exactly what to write at each stop, so you always know what's expected.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  You can write up to **20 journal entries** per Experience — one for each location you visit or prompt you respond to.
</Note>

## After the experience

When the field trip is over, your journal entries don't disappear — they become part of the full Experience log.

- Your teacher may **use your entries as a starting point for class discussion**, asking you to share observations from the field.
- Entries may feed into a **follow-up assessment or Study Guide** your teacher creates based on what the class documented.
- You can revisit your own entries at any time through the Experience activity in your classroom feed.

## Privacy and visibility

Your teacher is in full control of who can see your journal entries:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Private review" icon="lock">
    By default, entries may be held for your teacher to review before they become visible to the rest of the group. You'll be notified when your entry is approved.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Controlled sharing" icon="eye">
    Your teacher decides whether entries are shared with your classmates, parents, or kept between you and your teacher only. You won't be surprised by who can see your work.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  If you're unsure whether your entries are visible to your classmates, check with your teacher — they control the sharing settings for each Experience.
</Note>
