ChimeEdu is your classroom in a browser. Your teacher creates activities and posts them to your classroom feed — you complete them from any device, without needing to install anything. Everything your teacher assigns lives in one place, ready when you are.

## What you'll do on ChimeEdu

Depending on what your teacher sets up, you'll use ChimeEdu for a range of activities:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Join Live Quizzes" icon="bolt">
    Jump into real-time quiz sessions using a join code. Compete on the live leaderboard and see your results the moment you finish.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Complete Assessments" icon="clipboard-check">
    Submit formal assessments that are auto-graded and tracked in your teacher's gradebook. Multiple question types, instant feedback.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Explore Maps and Timelines" icon="map">
    Interact with pinned maps and sequenced timelines your teacher has built — great for history, geography, and any subject with a story.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Study with Flash Cards" icon="layer-group">
    Work through spaced-repetition flash card decks to lock in vocabulary and definitions at your own pace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Write Journal Entries" icon="pen-line">
    Document field trips and real-world experiences through guided journal prompts your teacher creates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Read Study Guides" icon="book-open">
    Access structured study guides your teacher has published — organised by topic and available any time from your classroom feed.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Do you need an account?

It depends on what you're doing:

- **For most activities** — yes. You need a ChimeEdu account to join a classroom, track your progress, and submit assessments.
- **For live quizzes** — you can join as a guest using just a join code. No account needed to play, but your results won't be saved to a gradebook.

<Note>
  Creating a student account is **free** — no credit card required. It takes less than two minutes.
</Note>
