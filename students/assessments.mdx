An assessment is formal work your teacher sets for you to complete on your own. Unlike a live quiz, you work at your own pace and your answers are saved for your teacher to review. Your submission is tied to your ChimeEdu account — you never type in your name.

## Starting an assessment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find it">
    Assessments your teacher has assigned appear on your dashboard under **My Assessments**, each with a badge — **New**, **Due today**, **Overdue**, **Missed**, or **Make-up open**. You can also open the link your teacher shared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    The assessment page shows who you're signed in as — your name, school email and avatar. On a shared or lab computer, check it's really you; there's a **Not you? Sign out** link if it isn't.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start">
    If you're enrolled in the classroom, one click starts the assessment. If you're signed in but not in that classroom yet, the same click enrolls you and starts it. If you're signed out, check your school email for your enrollment link, or sign in if you already have an account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **No account that works today?** If your teacher set up **password-only access** for you, you'll have an email with a link and a one-time password. The link expires 2 hours after your teacher created it. If you're under 13 and your account is still waiting for a parent's approval, this is how you take the assessment in the meantime.
</Note>

## Working through the questions

You might see any of five question types: multiple choice, true/false, image select (pick from a grid of pictures), short answer, and long answer with formatting.

Move between questions with **Previous** and **Next**. There's no overview screen showing which questions you've answered, so keep track as you go — and check back through before you submit.

### Timers

Your teacher may set either or both:

- An **overall timer** for the whole assessment. When it runs out, your assessment is submitted automatically.
- A **per-question timer**. When it runs out, you move on to the next question automatically.

If you have **extended time** as an accommodation, both timers are extended automatically — it's attached to your account, or to your access code if you're using password-only access.

### Due dates

If your teacher set a due date, it shows on the assessment page and your dashboard badge tracks it. After the due date, you can still submit only if your teacher allows late submissions — and only within their late window. After that the assessment closes; if you were absent, your teacher can open a personal **make-up window** for you.

### Pausing

Unless your teacher has required the assessment be done in one sitting, there's a **Pause** button that saves your progress so you can come back later — from any device, since your attempt is tied to your account.

<Warning>
  Your answers are saved when you pause and when you submit — not continuously. If you close the tab without pausing, answers you've typed since the last save can be lost. Pause before you walk away.
</Warning>

## Submitting

When you choose **Submit**, your assessment is final — you can't go back and change answers, and you can't retake it unless your teacher opens a make-up for you.

<Note>
  **Your results arrive when your teacher sends them.** After submitting you'll see a confirmation screen. Nothing is shown to you until your teacher releases your results — marking and releasing are separate steps, so a quiet gap doesn't mean you've been forgotten.
</Note>

## Seeing your results

When your teacher sends your results, three things happen at once:

- You get a **notification** in ChimeEdu.
- **Your results are ready** appears at the top of your dashboard, with a link straight to them.
- You get an **email** with your score and any comments.

Your results page shows your total score, the percentage, every question with the points you earned, which answer you chose (and the correct one, for multiple choice), plus any comment your teacher left on an individual answer and their overall feedback.

<Tip>
  Done with the dashboard prompt? **Dismiss** removes it from your dashboard — it doesn't delete your results. You can always get back to them from the notification.
</Tip>

<Note>
  If some questions are still being marked, you'll see **"Some questions are still being scored"** and no percentage. That's deliberate: a part-marked assessment shouldn't look like a final grade. Check back after your teacher finishes.
</Note>

Only you can open your results. The page is tied to your account, so a classmate can't reach your results by copying the link.

## Frequently asked

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an account to take an assessment?">
    Yes — you sign in and your submission is linked to your account. The exception is password-only access, which your teacher sets up for you when your account won't work (or is still waiting for parental approval).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says I'm not assigned to this assessment.">
    Your teacher chooses who each assessment is assigned to. If you think you should have it, ask them — they can assign you in a couple of clicks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says the assessment has closed.">
    The due date (plus any late window) has passed. Ask your teacher — for a legitimate absence they can open a make-up window just for you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Something went wrong when I hit Submit.">
    Wait a moment and press **Submit** again — a retry usually goes through, and it won't create a duplicate. If it still fails, reopen the assessment: your saved progress will be restored. Then tell your teacher what happened.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I submitted ages ago and still have no results.">
    Marking and releasing are separate steps — your teacher has to send results before you can see them, and nothing appears until they do. There's no way to tell from your side whether marking has started, so if it's been a while, just ask.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I dismissed the results prompt on my dashboard. Are my results gone?">
    No. Dismissing only clears the prompt. Open the notification about your results to get back to the page any time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My timer ran out while I was still working.">
    If it was the overall timer, your assessment was submitted as it stood. If it was a per-question timer, you were moved to the next question. Let your teacher know — they can see your submission and decide what to do.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
