An assessment is formal work your teacher sets for you to complete on your own. Unlike a live quiz, you work at your own pace and your answers are saved for your teacher to review.

## Starting an assessment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Find the assessment in the **Assessments** tab of your classroom page, or open the link your teacher shared.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the password">
    Every assessment is password-protected. Your teacher gives you the password when it's time to start.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your name">
    You'll be asked for your first and last name, and possibly an email address or student ID. Type your name the way your teacher expects — this is how they'll identify your work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you have extended time as an accommodation, **sign in to your ChimeEdu account before you start**. The extra time is attached to your account, so it won't apply if you take the assessment without signing in.
</Tip>

## Working through the questions

You might see any of five question types: multiple choice, true/false, image select (pick from a grid of pictures), short answer, and long answer with formatting.

Move between questions with **Previous** and **Next**. There's no overview screen showing which questions you've answered, so keep track as you go — and check back through before you submit.

### Timers

Your teacher may set either or both:

* An **overall timer** for the whole assessment. When it runs out, your assessment is submitted automatically.
* A **per-question timer**. When it runs out, you move on to the next question automatically.

Whichever is running is shown on screen as you work.

### Pausing

Unless your teacher has required the assessment be done in one sitting, there's a **Pause** button that saves your progress so you can come back later. Reopening the assessment in the same browser resumes where you left off.

<Warning>
  Your answers are saved when you pause and when you submit — not continuously. If you close the tab without pausing, answers you've typed since the last save can be lost. Pause before you walk away.
</Warning>

## Submitting

When you choose **Submit**, your assessment is final — you can't go back and change answers, and you can't retake it.

<Note>
  **You won't see a score.** After submitting you'll get a confirmation screen with a message from your teacher, and any overall feedback they've left. ChimeEdu doesn't show students their score or which questions they got right, and there's no way to reopen a submitted assessment to review it. Your teacher has your results — ask them if you'd like to know how you did.
</Note>

## Frequently asked

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an account to take an assessment?">
    Not to take one — the password and your name are what's required. But signing in matters if you have extended time, so sign in first if that applies to you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Something went wrong when I hit Submit.">
    Wait a moment and press **Submit** again — a retry usually goes through, and it won't create a duplicate. If it still fails, reopen the assessment: your saved progress will be restored. Then tell your teacher what happened.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My timer ran out while I was still working.">
    If it was the overall timer, your assessment was submitted as it stood. If it was a per-question timer, you were moved to the next question. Let your teacher know — they can see your submission and decide what to do.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When will I get my written answers back?">
    Written answers aren't scored automatically, and your teacher may leave comments on them rather than marks. There's no results page for you to check, so ask your teacher directly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
