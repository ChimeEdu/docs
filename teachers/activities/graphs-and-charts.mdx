Graphs & Charts activities turn your class into the dataset. Depending on the graph type you choose, students answer opinion statements, contribute measurements, or vote in a survey — and the results chart live, with each student appearing as their own avatar.

## The four graph types

One Graphs & Charts activity can be any of four types, chosen in the editor:

- **Compass** — students respond to your statements (Strongly Disagree → Strongly Agree, or number entry) and land as a dot on a two-axis quadrant chart, like a political-compass exercise. You define both axes, which direction each statement moves, and how much it counts.
- **Line** — you enter one or more data series (up to 8, each up to 500 points) and ChimeEdu draws them as connected lines. Great for trends, functions, and reading-a-graph practice. Students view the chart; they don't submit data.
- **Scatter** — plotted points from your data series, plus optional **class data collection**: each student submits one (X, Y) observation — height vs. shoe size, plant growth vs. sunlight — and appears on the chart as their avatar.
- **Pie** — a survey question with answer options you define. Each student votes once, and the class distribution charts as a pie or donut.

## Creating a Graphs & Charts activity

1. Go to **Activities → Create** and pick the **Graphs & Charts** tile.
2. Give it a title and link it to a classroom. The classroom link matters: students participate through their classroom enrollment.
3. In the editor, pick the graph type, then build your content — statements for a Compass, data series for Line/Scatter, or answer options for a Pie.

Settings save automatically as you work (the header shows the save status), and the live preview updates as you type. Statements, series, and options save with their own buttons.

## How students participate

Share the student link (or QR code) from the activity page. Students sign in with their classroom account — that's how their answer appears as _them_ on the chart. There's no anonymous participation for Graphs & Charts, and it isn't available as a Quick Activity.

Students can change their answer at any time; resubmitting replaces their previous response.

## Private vs. classroom results

Every participating graph type has a visibility setting:

- **Private** — each student sees only their own result. Only you see the full class.
- **Classroom** — after submitting, students see the whole class chart, with classmates shown as avatars (hover or focus a dot for the name and values).

Responses appear under each student's name, so consider your question topics and visibility mode together — an opinion compass on a sensitive topic may belong in Private mode, while a class measurement activity is a natural fit for Classroom sharing. The same reminder appears beneath the activity's Share box.

## Live results

Open **Live Results** from the activity page or editor for a projectable view that updates automatically as students respond. You can remove any individual submission from the roster — for example, your own after testing the student view.

## Save any chart as an image

Every chart — in the editor preview, the student view, and Live Results — has a **Save as image** button that downloads a PNG, ready to drop into homework, slides, or coursework submissions.

## Give each student their own chart

**Line** and **Scatter** charts can be issued **per student** on Plus and Premium, so each student authors their own data series instead of contributing to one class chart. Compass and Pie can't be issued this way — they already collect one submission per student. See [Per-Student & Groups](/teachers/activities/issuance).

## Accessibility

Every chart offers a **View as table** alternative with the exact underlying data, all data points are keyboard-focusable with tooltips, and charts respect reduced-motion preferences.
