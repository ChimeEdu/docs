A fill-in-the-blank activity is a passage with gaps for students to complete. Answers are graded automatically the moment a student submits, and each blank can accept several forms of the same answer.

<Note>
  In the app this activity type is labeled **Fill in the Blank**.
</Note>

## Building a passage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the activity">
    From your dashboard choose **New Activity**, then **Fill in the Blank**. Pick a classroom, or leave it as a temporary activity.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write or paste your passage">
    Use the passage editor to write the text. You can format with **bold**, *italic*, underline, links, and bulleted or numbered lists.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Insert your blanks">
    Put the cursor where a gap belongs and choose **Insert blank**. A numbered placeholder appears in the passage.

    <Warning>
      Inserting a blank does **not** capture the word you've selected — the placeholder is added at the cursor and the text stays as it is. Delete the word you're blanking out yourself, then type its answer in the blanks table (next step).
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the answers table">
    Below the passage, each numbered blank gets a row where you enter its **accepted answers**, an optional **hint**, and its **point value**. Separate multiple accepted answers with commas — matching ignores capitalization.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your settings, then save">
    Set whether to shuffle the passage order, whether hints are available to students, whether to show students their score, and a passing score if you want one. You can also password-protect the activity. Use **Preview** to try it as a student, then **Save passage** and **Save settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  One activity can hold several passages — use **Add passage** to build a multi-part exercise rather than creating separate activities.
</Tip>

## How answers are graded

Grading runs server-side as soon as a student submits.

* **Exact matches** (ignoring capitalization) against any of your accepted answers score full points.
* **Near misses score 75%.** ChimeEdu automatically allows small spelling slips — roughly one character for short answers, or up to a quarter of the answer's length for longer ones. The student sees *"Close!"* rather than a plain wrong. This tolerance is always on and can't be switched off, so bear it in mind when spelling itself is the thing you're testing.
* **Everything else** scores zero.

<Note>
  Matching is whole-answer, not partial. An answer with extra words around the target won't be accepted unless it falls inside the spelling tolerance — so if several phrasings are valid, list each one as an accepted answer.
</Note>

## Hints

If you add a hint to a blank, students can reveal it themselves from an icon beside that input. Hints are only available when the activity's **show hints** setting is on.

<Note>
  Using a hint doesn't cost the student any points, and hint usage isn't recorded.
</Note>

## Seeing student work

<Warning>
  **There is currently no teacher view for fill-in-the-blank results.** Student submissions are graded and stored, but no screen in the app displays them yet — the activity page shows only the passage and blank counts. If you need to see how students did, have them tell you their score (turn on **show score** so they see it), or use an [assessment](/teachers/assessments) for work you need to review.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do students get to the activity?">
    Once created, the activity is live — there's no separate publish step. It appears in the Activities tab of the classroom you assigned it to, and you can share its link directly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I add headings or images to the passage?">
    Not currently. The passage editor supports text formatting, links, and lists — headings and images aren't available in it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a limit on how many blanks a passage can have?">
    No fixed limit. Keep it readable — a passage that's more gap than text is hard to work through.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can the AI suggest which words to blank out?">
    Not today. Fill-in-the-blank activities are authored manually; ChimeEdu's only AI feature is [study guide drafting](/teachers/ai/study-guide-drafting).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can students take it without an account?">
    Yes — anyone with the link (and the password, if you set one) can complete the activity.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
