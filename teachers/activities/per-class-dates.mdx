When an activity is in more than one class, each class can open it and be due at its own time. Period 1 can start on Monday while Period 3 starts on Wednesday, and each class's students only ever see their own class's dates.

The dates live in one table, **Dates by class**, on the activity's **Students** section under **When**. You see the same table for an activity in a single class, with one row.

## The Dates by class table

There's one row for each class the activity is in:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Class** | The class name. The home class has a **Home** label |
| **Available from** | When the activity opens for that class. Empty means it's open now |
| **Due** | When work is due for that class. This column appears only when the activity is graded |
| **Status** | **Scheduled**, **Open**, **Closed** (graded and past its due date), or **Class archived**, with a short line such as *opens in 2 days* or *due today, 3:00 PM* |

The status updates by itself while the page is open.

## Change one class's dates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type the new date and time">
    Edit **Available from** or **Due** in that class's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave the row, or press Enter">
    The row saves when you move out of it or press **Enter** in one of its fields. Press **Escape** to put back the saved dates instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To open the activity for a class straight away, clear its **Available from** with the **✕** button.

**Available from** has to be before the due date. If a row has a problem, it keeps what you typed, shows a **Not saved** label and the reason, and sends nothing until you fix it.

## Set every class at once

Above the table, **Set every class at once** sets the same dates for every class:

1. Enter an **Available from** (or press **Open now**) and, if the activity is graded, a **Due** date.
2. Press **Apply to all** and confirm with **Apply**.
3. A line confirms *Dates applied to N classes*, with an **Undo** button that puts every class's previous dates back.

**Use for all** on a class's row copies that row's dates into **Set every class at once**, ready to apply. Archived classes are left out.

## Due dates and grading

While an activity is graded, **every** class needs a due date.

- When you turn grading on under **Results › Grading**, ChimeEdu asks **When is it due?** and sets that date for every class. Change it for individual classes in the table afterwards.
- With grading on, the **Due date & time** row under **Results › Grading** points to **Students › When** and shows the range of due dates.
- With grading off, the table has no **Due** column.

Students in a class can't submit after that class's due date, unless you reopen their work. See [Graded Mode](/teachers/activities/graded-mode).

## Time zones

Dates are exact moments, entered in your own local time. If a classroom is set to a different time zone from yours, its row also shows the time there, so you can check what its students will see.

## Show a locked preview

**Show a locked preview**, under the table, is one setting for the whole activity. It appears while at least one class is scheduled. When it's on, students in a class that hasn't opened yet see the activity listed as *Coming soon*, with its opening time, but they can't open it. When it's off, the activity stays hidden from them until it opens.

## Notices when a class opens

When a scheduled class's **Available from** time arrives, the students in that class get an in-app **Now open** notification that links to the activity. This applies to every scheduled activity, whether it's in one class or several.

- It's **in-app only**; no email is sent.
- Each student gets **at most one** "Now open" notice per activity. Moving the date and opening it again doesn't send another.
- Opening a class early, by clearing its **Available from** date or using **Open now**, sends the notice straight away.
- A student who's in two of the activity's classes hears only about the class they count in. See [Use One Activity in Several Classes](/teachers/activities/several-classes#students-in-two-of-your-classes).
- On a Per-Student or Group Assignment activity, only assigned students are notified, and in Group Assignment mode only students who are in a group.
- Adding a class to an activity that's already open sends nothing. Its students simply find it on their dashboard.

## What students see

- Students see **their own class's** dates on their dashboard, their classroom page, and in the activity.
- Someone who opens the activity from a class's classroom page link sees that class's dates.
- Before their class opens, students see nothing, or a *Coming soon* card if **Show a locked preview** is on.

## In the classic editor

The classic editor's **Available from** and due date fields still work while every class has the same dates. A note says they apply to all of the activity's classes, and saving them changes every class. Once the classes have different dates, those fields become read-only and point you to the **Dates by class** table.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do assessments and study guides have dates by class?">
    No. Assessments and study guides keep one **Available from** time for all their classrooms. See [Schedule Work and Post Announcements](/teachers/classrooms/schedule-and-announce).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I added a class. What dates does it get?">
    It starts with the home class's dates. Change them in its row.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does one row have no date fields?">
    That class is archived, and its row says **Class archived**. Restore the class to change its dates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I extend the due date for one class only?">
    Yes. Change the **Due** date in that class's row. To give one student more time, reopen their submission instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
