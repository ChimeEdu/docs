On a shared map where students add their own pins, you don't want Period 1 reading Period 3's work. As soon as a shared map is in **two or more classes**, it gets **class layers**:

- **Your pins** are seen by every class.
- **Each class's pins** are seen only by that class and by you.
- **Visitors**, meaning people who aren't students in one of the map's classes and add pins with the [Student Mode password](/teachers/activities/maps#let-students-add-their-own-pins), have a layer of their own. Only you and other visitors see it.

A map in one class works exactly as it always has. See [Use One Activity in Several Classes](/teachers/activities/several-classes) for adding classes.

## When class layers start

Class layers turn on the moment a shared map is linked to a second class, and they stay on. If you later remove classes, the pins stay in their layers; nothing is merged back.

## Who sees these pins? Choosing for existing pins

If the map already has pins that ChimeEdu can't confirm you added, adding a second class opens **Choose who sees each pin** before anything changes. That includes pins your students or visitors added, and older pins added before ChimeEdu recorded who added each pin. A map with only your own pins gets the class straight away.

The dialog lists every pin with its title, who added it, and a suggested place:

| The pin | Suggested place |
| --- | --- |
| You added it, it has no author name, or it has only your name | **Teacher (all classes)** |
| A student in one of the map's classes added it | That student's class |
| Someone who isn't in these classes added it | **Visitors** |
| It has other names typed in as authors | The home class |

Change any pin's **Who sees it** choice, or use the buttons at the top:

- **Use suggestions** puts every suggestion back.
- **All to teacher (every class)** shows every pin to every class.
- **All to** *home class* keeps every pin in the home class.

Press **Add class** to link the class and place the pins. **Cancel** adds nothing and changes nothing.

The dialog opens wherever you add the class: the activity's **Students** section or the classic editor's **Edit Details**. **Add to…** on your Activities list doesn't open it. Instead, that map is listed with **Choose who sees its pins first** and a **Review** link that takes you to its **Students** section.

## Viewing one class at a time

On a map with class layers, open the map in **Teacher mode** to see the **Showing** picker in the map header:

- **All classes**: every pin on the map
- **Teacher only**: just your pins
- each class by name, including classes marked *(archived)* or *(removed)* that still have pins
- **Visitors**: the visitors' layer, when it has pins

Each pin also carries a label for its layer, such as *Teacher · all classes*, *Period 3*, or *Visitors*. To add your own pins, set **Showing** to **All classes** or **Teacher only**. Pins you add always go to every class.

<Note>
  The **Showing** picker, the layer labels, and **Who sees this pin** appear only in **Teacher mode**.
</Note>

## Move one pin: Who sees this pin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pin in Teacher mode">
    Click the pin on the map in **Teacher mode**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who sees it">
    Under **Who sees this pin**, pick **Show to all classes**, **Keep in** *a class*, or **Visitors only**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Move">
    The pin moves to that layer straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On an area map or a map where students choose areas, each layer can hold only one entry per spot. If the spot is already taken in the layer you pick, ChimeEdu tells you which pin is there and leaves the pin where it was.

## Looking after pins

- You can edit or delete any pin on the map, in any class's layer or the visitors' layer.
- Only you can delete pins on a shared map. Students can't.
- On a map where students choose **areas**, each class fills its own areas. Two classes can both choose the same country, for example.

## What students see

- A student sees **your pins** and **their own class's pins**. Your pins are read-only for them.
- In **Student Mode**, a line under the map title tells them who sees what they add, for example *Pins you add are seen by Period 3 and your teacher.*
- A **visitor** sees your pins and the visitors' pins. Their line reads *Pins you add are seen by your teacher and other visitors.*

### Students in two of your classes

A student who's in two of the map's classes adds pins to the class they count in. They also see the pins **they** added earlier in their other class, marked *From your other class · view only*. They never see other students' pins from that class.

## Removing, archiving, and adding classes back

- **Removing a class** keeps its pins. Its layer shows as *(removed)* in the **Showing** picker, and adding the class back brings its students' pins back to them.
- **Archiving a class** keeps its pins too. While the class is archived, its students no longer see their class's pins, and you can't move pins into it. Restoring the class brings everything back.

## Per-student and group maps

Class layers are about shared maps. On a **Per-Student** or **Group Assignment** map in several classes, each student or group already has their own copy, and your pins show in it, locked. If you put any existing pins in one class when you added a class, only that class's students see them, also read-only. See [Per-Student & Groups](/teachers/activities/issuance).

## Duplicating and restoring a map with class layers

- **Duplicate** copies only the pins every class sees. Pins you kept in one class, each class's pins, and visitors' pins stay with the original. See [Duplicating](/teachers/activities/several-classes#duplicating).
- **Restore** from your Archived list keeps your own pins in the layer you kept them in, and clears students' and visitors' pins, so the map comes back fresh. See [Archive & Restore](/teachers/activities/archive-and-restore).

## Charts work the same way

Compass and Pie charts, and Scatter charts that collect data, show each class its own results once they're in several classes. See [Graphs & Charts](/teachers/activities/graphs-and-charts#results-for-each-class).
