If your school is not yet an institution on ChimeEdu, a school leader or IT person can bring it on board themselves. Open **Account settings → Your institution → Bring your school onto ChimeEdu**, or go to `app.chimeedu.com/institution/claim`.

## What you need first

Claiming a school is a high-trust action, so ChimeEdu checks a few things before it starts:

- an educator or administrator account (not a student account) with a confirmed email address;
- **two-step verification or a passkey** on your account;
- an email address on the school's domain;
- the school not already administered on ChimeEdu — if it is, ask its admin to invite you.

If anything is missing, the page says exactly what, with a link to fix it.

## Three ways to prove it

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="DNS record (recommended)" icon="network-wired">
    Add one TXT record at your DNS provider, then press **Check now**. Approval is automatic when the record is found.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Website file" icon="file-lines">
    Publish a small text file on the school website. Approval is automatic when the file is found.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manual review" icon="user-check">
    No DNS or website access? Point us at the public staff page that lists you and your role, plus a work phone. ChimeEdu reviews and emails you either way.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

A claim stays open for 14 days. You can withdraw it at any time from **Your claims** on the same page.

## After approval

You become the institution's first **Institution Admin**. Sign in at **staff.chimeedu.com** to walk through the [Setup Guide](/staff/setup-guide): verification, campuses, your look, your team, your teachers, network and library. Other admins of the institution (if any) are told that you verified control of the domain.
