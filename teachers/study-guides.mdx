A study guide is structured reference content for your students — study notes, a topic overview, an exam primer. You build it from blocks, publish it to your classrooms, and students read it in a clean, accessible reading view.

<Note>
  Study Guides are **available on the Premium plan** (paid or comp'd) — creating one requires Premium. A study guide you've already created stays open to view and edit if you later move to a lower tier; only creating a _new_ one is gated. See [Plans & Pricing](/teachers/plans-and-pricing).
</Note>

## Building a guide

Choose **New Study Guide** from your dashboard, then assemble the page from blocks:

| Block | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Heading** | Section titles — these become the entries in the contents list |
| **Paragraph** | Body text with bold, italic, underline, links, and lists |
| **Image** | Pictures with a caption and alt text |
| **Video** | YouTube or Vimeo embeds |
| **Callout** | Highlighted notes, warnings, and key points |
| **Table** | Structured comparisons and data |
| **Divider** | A visual break between topics |
| **Embed** | Other embeddable content |

<Note>
  Blocks sit in a single flat sequence — there are no nested subsections, and the contents list is a flat set of links to your heading blocks. Structure a long guide by using headings consistently and keeping the order logical, or by splitting it into several guides.
</Note>

## Google Drive resources

On a Plus or Premium account you can attach Google Drive files to a study guide. Files are **linked, not copied** — students always open the current version in Drive, and ChimeEdu never modifies your Drive content.

<Note>
  Attachments are listed for the guide as a whole rather than embedded inside particular sections, and the file name is captured when you attach it — renaming the file in Drive later won't update the name shown in ChimeEdu.
</Note>

## AI-assisted drafting

Premium accounts can draft a guide with AI: choose **Generate with AI**, describe what you want, optionally upload source material, and watch sections stream in. You can refine with follow-up instructions, regenerate individual sections, and append to or replace existing content. See [Study Guide AI](/teachers/ai/study-guide-drafting) for the full workflow.

<Warning>
  AI output is written straight into the guide as it completes — there's no separate draft to approve first. If the guide is already published, students see the new content immediately. Draft in an unpublished guide when you want to review before students do.
</Warning>

## Publishing and access

A guide is a **draft** until you publish it. Publishing makes it available in the **Study Guides** tab of every classroom you've linked it to.

<Warning>
  Published guides are reachable by **anyone with the classroom URL** — enrollment isn't checked, and the page offers a copy-link button for sharing. If a guide shouldn't be public, set a password on it; that password is the only access control available. Keep genuinely sensitive material off study guides.
</Warning>

## What students get

Students read the guide with a set of reading controls they can adjust themselves:

- A **contents** list for jumping between sections
- **Font choice**, including **OpenDyslexic** for readers with dyslexia
- Their preferences are remembered on their device

No action is needed from you to enable these.

## Frequently asked

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I upload a video file directly?">
    Study guide video blocks take YouTube and Vimeo links. Direct video file upload is restricted to platform administrators.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I link one guide to several classrooms?">
    Yes — a study guide can be linked to as many of your classrooms as you like, and it appears in each one's Study Guides tab.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do study guides count toward my plan limit?">
    No. Your plan's limit counts activities and assessments; study guides and Experiences sit outside it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does publishing notify my students?">
    No — publishing doesn't send an email or in-app alert. Let your class know it's ready.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
