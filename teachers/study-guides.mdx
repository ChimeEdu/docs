A study guide is structured reference content for your students — study notes, a topic overview, an exam primer. You build it from blocks, publish it to your classrooms, and students read it in a clean, accessible reading view.

<Note>
  Study Guides are **available on the Premium plan** (paid or comp'd) — creating one requires Premium. A study guide you've already created stays open to view and edit if you later move to a lower tier; only creating a _new_ one is gated. See [Plans & Pricing](/teachers/plans-and-pricing).
</Note>

## Your study guides

**Study Guides** in the sidebar lists every guide you've made.

- **Pick up where you left off** shows the three you edited most recently.
- **Search** by title, **filter** by class (including guides that aren't in a class yet), and **sort** by most recently edited, title, or newest.
- The **All**, **Published** and **Draft** chips show how many guides are in each.
- Switch between **cards** and a **list**. Phones always show cards.
- Each guide's **⋯** menu offers Open, Preview as a student, Copy the student link, Print, Classes…, Publish or Unpublish, Duplicate, and Delete.
- Choose **Select** to work on several guides at once: add them to a class, remove them from a class, publish, unpublish, or delete.

## Making a guide

Choose **New study guide** on the Study Guides page or your dashboard. Pick how to start:

- **Blank page**: you add the blocks yourself.
- **Draft it with AI**: the AI assistant opens on the new guide so you can describe what you want.

Give it a title and, optionally, a description and the classes it belongs in. A new guide always starts as a draft.

## The study guide page

Each guide has four tabs:

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Build** | The blocks themselves, with the contents list beside them. Switch to **Preview** to see the page as students will. |
| **Students** | Which **classes** can see it, when it **opens** (if you schedule it), and whether students need a **password**. |
| **Share** | A QR code and link for each class, with Open, Download QR and Email. There's also **Print** and **Preview**. |
| **Details** | Title and description, cover image, image gallery, Google Drive files, and standards. |

- The **Ready for students?** menu at the top lists anything left to do: for example, adding the guide to a class or describing an image. Each item takes you to the tab where you fix it.
- While the guide is a draft, **Publish** sits next to it.
- The status line shows whether your changes have saved. Blocks save automatically a few seconds after you stop typing.
- Settings on the Students and Details tabs open in a panel with its own **Save** button.

<Note>
  Every class has its own student link. A guide in several classes has one link per class: choose the class on the **Share** tab, or from **Copy the student link** in the guide's ⋯ menu.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Prefer the previous layout? Choose **Open the classic page** from the guide's **⋯** menu. To come back, go to **Account Settings → Preferences → Editor** and choose **New Studio**. The choice is saved per browser and covers all the redesigned pages.
</Tip>

## Building a guide

On the **Build** tab, assemble the page from blocks with **Insert block**:

| Block | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Heading** | Section titles — these become the entries in the contents list |
| **Paragraph** | Body text with bold, italic, underline, links, and lists |
| **Image** | Pictures with a caption and alt text |
| **Video** | YouTube or Vimeo embeds |
| **Callout** | Highlighted notes, warnings, and key points |
| **Table** | Structured comparisons and data |
| **Divider** | A visual break between topics |
| **Embed** | Your ChimeEdu activities, assessments, and Experiences — see below |

<Note>
  Blocks sit in a single flat sequence — there are no nested subsections, and the contents list is a flat set of links to your heading blocks. Structure a long guide by using headings consistently and keeping the order logical, or by splitting it into several guides.
</Note>

## Embedding activities

Choose **Embed Activity** from the **Insert block** menu to place your own content inside the guide. Everything except Live Quizzes can be embedded, and each type behaves in one of three ways on the published page:

- **Flash Cards** and **Fill-in-the-Blank** run as compact interactive players directly in the guide — students flip cards or fill blanks without leaving the page.
- **Maps, Timelines, Graphs & Charts, and Diagrams** appear as a card with a **Load** button; clicking it opens the full interactive activity in place. An **Open full page** link is always available too.
- **Assessments** and **Experiences** appear as link cards that open on their own page in a new tab.

<Note>
  Embeds follow the activity's own access rules. An archived activity shows its archived notice, a graded activity keeps its due-date and submission behavior, and an assessment still requires the student to be enrolled (or have a password link) when they open it. Only **published** assessments can be embedded.
</Note>

## Google Drive resources

On a Plus or Premium account you can attach Google Drive files to a study guide. Files are **linked, not copied** — students always open the current version in Drive, and ChimeEdu never modifies your Drive content.

<Note>
  Attachments are listed for the guide as a whole rather than embedded inside particular sections, and the file name is captured when you attach it — renaming the file in Drive later won't update the name shown in ChimeEdu.
</Note>

## AI-assisted drafting

Premium accounts can draft a guide with AI: choose **Generate with AI** on the Build tab, describe what you want, optionally upload source material, and watch sections stream in. You can refine with follow-up instructions, regenerate individual sections, and append to or replace existing content. See [Study Guide AI](/teachers/ai/study-guide-drafting) for the full workflow.

<Warning>
  AI output is written straight into the guide as it completes — there's no separate draft to approve first. If the guide is already published, students see the new content immediately. Draft in an unpublished guide when you want to review before students do.
</Warning>

## Publishing and access

A guide is a **draft** until you publish it. Publishing makes it available in every classroom you've linked it to; you choose those classes on the guide's **Students** tab. If you set an opening date there, students can open it from that date. You can also let them see the title beforehand.

<Warning>
  Published guides are reachable by **anyone with the classroom URL** — enrollment isn't checked, and the page offers a copy-link button for sharing. If a guide shouldn't be public, set a password on it (**Students → Access**); that password is the only access control available. Keep genuinely sensitive material off study guides.
</Warning>

## Archiving and restoring

When a unit is over, **archive** a guide instead of deleting it. Choose **Archive…** from its **⋯** menu, on the list or on the guide's page.

- **Students can't open it.** It leaves your classes' pages, and an old link shows a notice that the guide has been archived.
- **Everything is kept**, including its content and its classes.
- **Find it again** with the **Archived** filter on the Study Guides list. There you can **Restore** it, **Duplicate** it or delete it.
- **Restore** puts it back exactly as it was: same classes, same published or draft status.
- While it's archived the guide is read-only. Restore it to make changes.

<Note>
  Archived study guides are kept for **365 days** after you archive them, then deleted for good.
</Note>

## What students get

Students read the guide with a set of reading controls they can adjust themselves:

- A **contents** list for jumping between sections
- **Font choice**, including **OpenDyslexic** for readers with dyslexia
- Their preferences are remembered on their device

No action is needed from you to enable these.

## Frequently asked

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I upload a video file directly?">
    Study guide video blocks take YouTube and Vimeo links. Direct video file upload is restricted to platform administrators.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I link one guide to several classrooms?">
    Yes — a study guide can be linked to as many of your classrooms as you like, and it appears in each one's Study Guides tab.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do study guides count toward my plan limit?">
    No. Your plan's limit counts activities and assessments; study guides and Experiences sit outside it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does publishing notify my students?">
    No — publishing doesn't send an email or in-app alert. Let your class know it's ready.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
