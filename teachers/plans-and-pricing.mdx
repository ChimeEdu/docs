ChimeEdu is built on a simple philosophy: every teacher should be able to start creating without a barrier. That means the Free tier is genuinely useful — no trial period, no credit card, no feature that disappears after 14 days. When your classroom needs grow beyond what Free offers, Plus and Premium are there to give you more room and more powerful tools. You can review the full comparison at [app.chimeedu.com/pricing](https://app.chimeedu.com/pricing).

### About the limits

<Accordion title="What counts as an Activity or Assessment?">
  Saved **activities** (quizzes, maps, timelines, flash card decks, and fill-in-the-blank activities) and **assessments** count toward your plan's limit. **Study guides and Experiences do not count** — they aren't part of the calculation. **Temporary activities** — those created without an account, or set to expire after 8 hours — don't count either, and are unlimited on every plan.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is Google Drive Integration?">
  Plus and Premium accounts can connect a Google account to ChimeEdu. The connection is per-teacher and works with any Google account — it isn't an institution-wide integration, and connecting yours doesn't connect your colleagues'. Once linked, you can attach files and folders to classrooms, activities, assessments, study guides, and Experiences. ChimeEdu links to your files rather than copying them, and can only ever see the specific files and folders you pick in the Google picker — never the rest of your Drive.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What are AI-Powered Activity Tools?">
  Premium accounts can use AI-assisted **study guide drafting** (powered by Claude): describe the guide you want, optionally upload source material, and the AI drafts sections into your study guide for you to edit. Study guides are the only place AI authoring is available — there is no AI question generation for quizzes or fill-in-the-blank activities. You always stay the author.
</Accordion>

## Which plan is right for you?

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="I'm just getting started">
    **Start with Free.** The Free tier is fully functional — you get one classroom and five saved activities, plus unlimited temporary activities for quick one-off sessions. There's no time pressure and no credit card required. Explore the platform, build your first quiz or map, and worry about limits only when you actually reach them.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="I teach multiple classes">
    **Consider Plus.** If you teach more than one class group and want to keep activities organized by classroom, Plus gives you five classrooms and fifty activities — enough for most full-time teachers running three to five subjects. Google Drive integration also means you won't have to manage files in two places.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="I want AI tools and maximum capacity">
    **Go with Premium.** Premium gives you 25 classrooms, 100 activities, Google Drive integration, and access to AI-powered authoring tools. It's the right fit for department leads, schools adopting ChimeEdu platform-wide, or teachers who want to move fast with AI-assisted content creation.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Not sure which plan you'll need long-term? Start on Free. Changing tier doesn't disturb anything you've built — your classrooms and activities carry over untouched. From August 17, 2026 you can upgrade, switch, or cancel yourself at any time — see [Billing & Subscriptions](/teachers/billing-and-subscriptions).
</Tip>
