ChimeEdu is built on a simple philosophy: every teacher should be able to start creating without a barrier. That means the Free tier is genuinely useful — no trial period, no credit card, no feature that disappears after 14 days. When your classroom needs grow beyond what Free offers, Plus and Premium are there to give you more room and more powerful tools. You can review the full comparison at [app.chimeedu.com/pricing](https://app.chimeedu.com/pricing).

### About the limits

<Accordion title="What counts as an Activity or Assessment?">
  Each quiz, map, timeline, flash card deck, assessment, study guide, experience, and fill-in-the-blank activity you save to a classroom counts toward your plan's activity limit. **Temporary activities** — those created without an account or set to expire after 8 hours — do not count toward this limit and are unlimited on every plan.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is Google Drive Integration?">
  Plus and Premium accounts can connect their institution's Google Drive to ChimeEdu. Once linked, you can attach files and folders directly to classrooms, study guides, and experiences — files stay current without duplicating them, and students always access the latest version.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What are AI-Powered Activity Tools?">
  Premium accounts have access to ChimeEdu's AI tools (powered by Claude), which can assist with drafting study guides from your own notes, suggesting fill-in-the-blank questions from a passage, and structuring curriculum content. You always stay the author — AI does the scaffolding, not the teaching.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is ChimeBot Support?">
  ChimeBot is ChimeEdu's built-in support assistant, available to all users on every plan. It can answer questions about platform features, guide you through common tasks, and direct you to the right Knowledge Base articles.
</Accordion>

## Which plan is right for you?

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="I'm just getting started">
    **Start with Free.** The Free tier is fully functional — you get one classroom and five saved activities, plus unlimited temporary activities for quick one-off sessions. There's no time pressure and no credit card required. Explore the platform, build your first quiz or map, and upgrade only when you know you need more.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="I teach multiple classes">
    **Consider Plus.** If you teach more than one class group and want to keep activities organized by classroom, Plus gives you five classrooms and fifty activities — enough for most full-time teachers running three to five subjects. Google Drive integration also means you won't have to manage files in two places.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="I want AI tools and maximum capacity">
    **Go with Premium.** Premium gives you 25 classrooms, 100 activities, Google Drive integration, and access to AI-powered authoring tools. It's the right fit for department leads, schools adopting ChimeEdu platform-wide, or teachers who want to move fast with AI-assisted content creation.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Not sure which plan you'll need long-term? Start on Free and upgrade once you hit the limits. ChimeEdu is designed so that moving to a higher tier is seamless — all your existing classrooms and activities carry over.
</Tip>
