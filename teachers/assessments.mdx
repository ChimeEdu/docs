Assessments are ChimeEdu's tool for formal, recorded work. Unlike a live quiz, an assessment is taken independently by each student and every submission is stored for you to review afterwards.

## Question types

There are five:

| Type                        | Auto-graded | Notes                        |
| --------------------------- | ----------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Multiple Choice**         | Yes         | Up to 6 options              |
| **True/False**              | Yes         |                              |
| **Image Select**            | Yes         | Up to 4 images in a 2×2 grid |
| **Short Answer**            | No          | Free-text response           |
| **Long Answer (Rich Text)** | No          | Formatted extended response  |

<Warning>
  **Written questions cannot currently be given a score.** Short Answer and Long Answer responses are not auto-graded, and there is no way for you to award points to them — you can leave comments on a response and overall feedback on the submission, but the points stay at zero while the question still counts toward the total. An assessment that mixes written and objective questions will therefore always report less than 100%. Until this changes, either keep written questions in **submission-only** assessments, or treat the reported percentage as covering the objective questions alone.
</Warning>

## Building an assessment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    From your dashboard choose **New Assessment**, give it a title and description, and optionally add a featured image or icon.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a password">
    Every assessment has a password — it's required, not optional. Students enter it before they can begin, so share it when you're ready for them to start.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose how it's scored">
    **Grade & score** runs the auto-grader on objective questions when a student submits. **Submission only** collects responses without scoring — the better fit for reflective or written work.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your questions">
    Mix the five types freely. Each question can carry images, and you set its point value.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set timing">
    You can set an **overall time limit** in minutes, which auto-submits when it expires, and/or a **per-question limit** in seconds, which moves the student on automatically. You can also require the assessment be completed in one sitting.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview, then publish">
    Preview runs the whole assessment without creating a submission. Publishing lists it in the **Assessments** tab of any classroom you've linked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Publishing doesn't notify anyone — there's no email or in-app alert to students. Tell your class it's ready, and share the password.
</Note>

## How students take it

Students open the assessment, enter the **password**, then enter their **first and last name** (optionally an email address and a student ID), and begin.

<Warning>
  Identity is self-reported. Submissions are not linked to ChimeEdu student accounts or to classroom enrollment — a student types their name, and that name is what appears on the submission. Ask your class to use a consistent format so you can match submissions to your roster.
</Warning>

Students can **Pause** to save progress and return later, unless you've required one sitting. If they close the tab without pausing, unsaved answers on the current question are lost, though reopening the assessment resumes their saved progress.

## Reviewing submissions

The assessment page lists submissions with a status of **In progress**, **Submitted**, or **Graded**. Open one to see the student's answers, add a comment on any individual response, and leave overall feedback — which does appear to the student.

You can export the submission list to CSV for your own records or gradebook.

<Note>
  The submission list doesn't update by itself — refresh the page to pull in new arrivals. And because submissions only exist once a student has started, there's no "not yet started" list to check against your roster.
</Note>

## Accessibility and accommodations

Students with **extended time** enabled on their roster entry get triple the time on **per-question** timers.

<Warning>
  Two limits to plan around: extended time does **not** apply to the overall assessment timer, and it only applies to students who are **signed in** — because the flag lives on their account. A student who takes an assessment without signing in won't get their accommodation. Ask students entitled to extra time to sign in before starting, and prefer per-question timers when accommodations matter.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can students see their score?">
    Not in ChimeEdu. After submitting, a student sees a confirmation screen with your confirmation message and any overall feedback you've left — but no score or per-question breakdown, and there's no way for them to return and review right and wrong answers. Share results yourself if students need them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a matching question type?">
    No. The five types listed above are the complete set.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there per-question analytics?">
    Not currently. You can see each submission individually and export the list to CSV, but ChimeEdu doesn't calculate class-wide per-question statistics.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can open a published assessment?">
    Anyone with the classroom URL can see it listed, and anyone with the assessment password can take it. Enrollment isn't checked — the password is the access control.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do assessments count toward my plan limit?">
    Yes. Your plan's limit covers activities and assessments together.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
