Two tools for running a class week to week: **scheduled release** lets you build work ahead of time and have it open by itself, and **announcements** let you tell the whole class something without leaving ChimeEdu.

<Note>
  If you don't see the *Available from* field or the Announcements card described here, they haven't been switched on yet.
</Note>

## Scheduled release: "Available from"

Any published assessment, activity or study guide can carry an **Available from** date and time. Until that moment, students can't open it, and it doesn't appear on their dashboard or the classroom page at all — so a Monday-morning quiz stays a surprise until Monday morning.

### Where to set it

- **Assessments** — open the assessment, click **Edit**, and find *Available from* in the settings panel next to the due date.
- **Activities** — open the activity, click **Edit**, and use the *Availability* section. It's there for every activity type.
- **Study guides** — the *Availability* card on the study guide page has its own **Save**.

Times are entered in your own local time. Leave the field blank to keep something available as soon as it's published, and clear it later to open something early. An *Available from* time has to come before the due date when both are set.

### Allow preview

Tick **Allow preview** if you'd like students to know something is coming. They then see a locked card with the title, description and opening time — but nothing inside, and nothing they can click. Leave it unticked and the item is simply invisible until it opens.

### What you see, what students see

| | You | Students |
|---|---|---|
| Before the opening time | A **Scheduled** chip on the classroom tabs; you can open and edit as usual | Nothing, or a locked *Opens Mon 9:00* card if you allowed preview |
| At the opening time | The chip disappears | The item appears and opens. For assessments, the "new assessment" notification and email go out **now**, not when you published |

<Tip>
  Because the notification waits for the opening time, you can publish a whole unit on Sunday evening and nobody hears about Friday's test until Friday.
</Tip>

## Announcements

The **Announcements** card sits at the top of the classroom's Overview tab. Write a note, press **Post**, and every enrolled student gets an in-app notification straight away. Students who have an email address also get one email per announcement, unless they've turned that off in their settings; students with a teacher-created username-only account see it in ChimeEdu only.

- **Pin** keeps one announcement at the top of the list. Pinning another one un-pins the first.
- **Edit** changes the text and marks the post *edited*. Editing doesn't send a new notification.
- **Delete** removes the post. Students who already got the notification keep it.
- **Show older** pages back through past posts, newest first.

Formatting is deliberately simple — bold, italic, underline, lists and links. Announcements are limited to 5,000 characters, and to 20 posts an hour.

### Where students read them

- On their **dashboard**, an _Announcements_ section at the top shows the latest post from each class, and every classroom card carries the latest one as a coloured callout. Clicking either opens the classroom.
- On the **classroom page**, the announcement stream sits above the content tabs, pinned first.
- In the **notification bell**, and by email if they've kept that on.

Announcements are class content: only you and your enrolled students can see them. Anyone else who opens the classroom's public link sees the classroom without the stream.

## Frequently asked

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I schedule an announcement?">
    Not yet — announcements post immediately. For work that should appear later, use *Available from* on the item itself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I set an opening time in the past by mistake.">
    A past time means "available now". Nothing breaks; the item is simply open.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does scheduling change the due date or the close?">
    No. *Available from* only controls when work opens. Due dates, late windows and the hard close work exactly as before.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can students reply to an announcement?">
    No. A student who needs to reach you uses **Contact teacher** from their dashboard.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

See also [Archive and Restore a Classroom](/teachers/classrooms/archive-a-classroom) and [Assessments](/teachers/assessments) for due dates and late windows.
