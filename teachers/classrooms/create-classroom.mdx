Creating a classroom is the first step to organizing your teaching on ChimeEdu. Once your classroom is set up, every activity, assessment, study guide, and experience you publish will appear automatically in your students' feed — no extra sharing required. The whole process takes less than a minute.

## Create a classroom

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to your ChimeEdu account">
    Go to [app.chimeedu.com](https://app.chimeedu.com/login) and sign in with your teacher account. You'll land on your dashboard, which shows all your existing classrooms and recent activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Classroom">
    From your dashboard, click the **Create Classroom** button. This opens the classroom creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your classroom">
    Give your classroom a clear, descriptive name. A good name makes it easy for you to manage multiple classrooms and helps students immediately recognize which class they're looking at.

    See [Organizing your classrooms](#organizing-your-classrooms) below for naming tips.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your classroom is ready">
    ChimeEdu creates your classroom instantly. You'll be taken directly to your new classroom, where you can start publishing content or customize settings straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the join link with your students">
    Copy the classroom join link and share it with your students. Once they click the link and enrol, they'll have access to everything you publish in this classroom going forward.

    See [Enroll Students](/teachers/classrooms/enroll-students) for the full enrollment guide.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can create as many classrooms as your plan allows. **Free** accounts include 1 classroom. **Plus** (coming soon) includes 5, and **Premium** (coming soon) includes 25. To create additional classrooms, upgrade your plan from your account settings.
</Note>

## Organizing your classrooms

ChimeEdu gives you full flexibility in how you name and structure your classrooms. Choose a convention that matches the way you already think about your timetable:

- **By subject** — e.g., *Year 9 History*, *A-Level Biology*, *English Literature*
- **By year group** — e.g., *Year 7 Tutor Group*, *Grade 10 Core*
- **By period or block** — e.g., *Period 3 — Geography*, *Block B Science*
- **By term** — e.g., *Spring Term Chemistry*, *Semester 1 Maths*

<Tip>
  You can create multiple classrooms for the same subject — for example, one classroom per period if you teach the same course to different groups. Each classroom has its own independent enrollment, activity feed, and submission tracking, so nothing crosses between your classes.
</Tip>

## Publishing content to a classroom

Once your classroom exists, publishing content to it is automatic. Whenever you create a new activity, assessment, study guide, or experience and assign it to a classroom, it appears immediately in the feed for every enrolled student — no manual sharing step needed.

Students who enrol after you've published content can still access everything you've already published. The full feed is always visible to all enrolled students.
