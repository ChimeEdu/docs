Classrooms are the central hub of everything you teach on ChimeEdu. Every activity you create — quizzes, timelines, flash cards, map activities, fill-in-the-blank exercises, assessments, study guides, and experiences — links directly to a classroom and becomes instantly accessible to every student enrolled in it. Instead of sharing a separate link for each resource, you share a classroom once and your students always find the right content in the right place.

## What lives in a classroom

A classroom on ChimeEdu is more than a folder. It's the live connection between you, your content, and your students. Anything you link to a classroom appears on its page straight away — there's no separate publishing step.

| Content type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Activities** | Maps, quizzes, timelines, flash cards, fill-in-the-blank exercises, graphs & charts, and diagrams you assign to the class |
| **Assessments** | Formal assessments with auto-scored question types, access by enrollment, and a list of submissions you can open individually |
| **Study Guides** | Structured reference material with rich text, sections, and images for students to review |
| **Experiences** | Documented field trips you write — locations, journal posts, staff profiles, and resources for students to read |

Content is grouped into tabs — All, Activities, Assessments, Study Guides, and Experiences — and listed newest first, with search and sorting available to students.

<Note>
  A classroom page sits on a public URL. Enrollment decides whose dashboard it appears on, not who can open the link — so treat the URL as shareable. Assessments open only to enrolled students, and study guide passwords give you access control for guides. Experiences set to **restricted** are hidden from the classroom listing entirely.
</Note>

## Key features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Everything in one place" icon="list-check">
    Everything you link to the classroom appears on its page, newest first and grouped by type. Students open the classroom and see what's there — no hunting for links.
  </Card>
  <Card title="One-Step Sharing" icon="share-nodes">
    Share your classroom code with your students once. Everything you link to the classroom after that appears for them automatically — no extra steps required.

  <Card title="Reusable across classrooms" icon="link">
    Activities, assessments, study guides, and Experiences can all be linked to more than one classroom at a time, so you can share the same material with several class groups.   </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Classroom limits by plan

The number of classrooms you can create depends on your ChimeEdu subscription plan.

| Plan | Classrooms |
|---|---|
| **Free** | 1 |
| **Plus** | 5 |
| **Premium** | 25 |

<Note>
  The Free plan includes 1 classroom and up to 5 activities and assessments — enough to get started with no credit card required. You can upgrade to Plus or Premium yourself at any time. See [Plans and Pricing](/teachers/plans-and-pricing) for details.
</Note>
