Classrooms are the central hub of everything you teach on ChimeEdu. Every activity you create — quizzes, timelines, flash cards, map activities, fill-in-the-blank exercises, assessments, study guides, and experiences — links directly to a classroom and becomes instantly accessible to every student enrolled in it. Instead of sharing a separate link for each resource, you share a classroom once and your students always find the right content in the right place.

## What lives in a classroom

A classroom on ChimeEdu is more than a folder. It's the live connection between you, your content, and your students. Every piece of content you publish flows into a single, ordered activity feed that students see the moment they log in.

| Content type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Activities** | Maps, quizzes, timelines, flash cards, and fill-in-the-blank exercises you assign to the class |
| **Assessments** | Formal, auto-graded assessments with per-question analytics and submission tracking |
| **Study Guides** | Structured reference material with rich text, sections, and images for students to review |
| **Experiences** | Documented field trips with map routes, student journal entries, and shareable summaries |

All four content types appear together in a single chronological feed — students never need to switch views or hunt for links.
