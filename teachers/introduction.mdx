ChimeEdu is an interactive K-12 learning platform built for teachers — and designed in collaboration with them. Whether you want to run a live, competitive quiz, build a richly detailed map activity, publish a structured study guide, or send students on a documented field trip, ChimeEdu gives you all of those tools in one place. This guide introduces you to what the platform can do, how it's organized, and how to get the most out of your account.

## What you can build

## How ChimeEdu works

The **Classroom** is your organizational hub on ChimeEdu. Each Classroom groups together a set of students and all the content you've built for them — quizzes, maps, timelines, flash cards, fill-in-the-blank activities, graphs & charts, diagrams, assessments, study guides, and experiences. When you link an activity to a Classroom it appears there straight away — there's no separate publish step. Students log in, open their Classroom, and see what's there, grouped by type and listed newest first. You can run multiple Classrooms at once (subject to your plan tier), each with its own enrollment and content.
