ChimeEdu is an interactive K-12 learning platform built for teachers — and designed in collaboration with them. Whether you want to run a live, competitive quiz, build a richly detailed map activity, publish a structured study guide, or send students on a documented field trip, ChimeEdu gives you all of those tools in one place. This guide introduces you to what the platform can do, how it's organized, and how to get the most out of your account.

## How ChimeEdu works

The **Classroom** is your organizational hub on ChimeEdu. Each Classroom groups together a set of students and all the content you've built for them — quizzes, maps, timelines, assessments, study guides, experiences, and fill-in-the-blank activities. When you publish an activity to a Classroom, it appears instantly in every enrolled student's feed. Students log in, open their Classroom, and see exactly what they need — no separate links, no hunting around. You can run multiple Classrooms at once (subject to your plan tier), each with its own enrollment, activity feed, and submission tracking.
