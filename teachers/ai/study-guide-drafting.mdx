The Study Guide AI assistant takes content you paste in and helps scaffold it into a structured, student-ready draft. Instead of starting from a blank page, you start from your own material — and the AI helps you shape it into something your students can navigate and study from effectively.

<Note>
  AI-assisted Study Guide drafting requires a **Premium** account. Powered by Claude. [View pricing](https://app.chimeedu.com/pricing).
</Note>

## Using AI to draft a Study Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open or create a Study Guide">
    From your classroom dashboard, open an existing Study Guide or create a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate with AI">
    Select the **Generate with AI** option in the Study Guide editor toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste in your source material">
    Paste in your notes, a curriculum document, or a textbook excerpt. The more context you provide, the better the draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the AI-generated structure and content">
    The AI will return a draft with suggested headings, sections, and expanded content. Read through the full draft before making any changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit, add, remove, and refine">
    Use the full rich text editor to adjust the AI's output. Add your own examples, remove anything that doesn't fit, and rewrite sections to match your voice and curriculum.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish when ready">
    Once you're satisfied with the guide, publish it to your classroom. It will appear immediately in your enrolled students' activity feeds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the AI does

The Study Guide AI is focused on structure and clarity. Specifically, it:

- **Suggests section headings** — it reads your pasted content and proposes a logical hierarchy of topics and subtopics.
- **Expands bullet points into paragraphs** — short notes become readable, student-friendly prose.
- **Organises content into hierarchical sections** — related ideas are grouped together so students can navigate complex topics without losing their place.

<Tip>
  Paste in multiple sources at once — for example, your own lesson notes alongside a relevant curriculum document — for a more complete and well-rounded draft.
</Tip>

## What the AI doesn't do

It's equally important to know the AI's limits:

- **It doesn't invent facts.** The AI only works from the content you paste in. It will not add information, statistics, or claims that weren't in your source material.
- **It doesn't publish automatically.** Nothing is sent to students without your explicit action. You always review and publish.
- **It doesn't replace your judgement.** The draft is a starting point. You decide what's accurate, appropriate, and pedagogically sound for your students.

<Note>
  You can **Accept** or **Dismiss** individual AI suggestions inline within the editor — you don't have to accept or reject the entire draft at once.
</Note>
