The Fill-in-the-Blank AI assistant helps you decide which words in a passage are the best candidates for blanks — based on conceptual importance. Rather than reading through a passage and manually hunting for the right words to remove, you get a set of reasoned suggestions you can evaluate, accept, or dismiss one by one.

<Note>
  AI-assisted Fill-in-the-Blank authoring requires a **Premium** account. Powered by Claude. [View pricing](https://app.chimeedu.com/pricing).
</Note>

## Using AI for Fill-in-the-Blank

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create or open a Fill-in-the-Blank activity">
    From your classroom dashboard, create a new Fill-in-the-Blank activity or open an existing one you want to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste in your passage">
    Paste the text you want to turn into a cloze-style activity. This can be your own writing, a textbook excerpt, or any passage from your curriculum materials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click AI Suggest Blanks">
    Select the **AI Suggest Blanks** option. The AI will analyse your passage and return a list of suggested words to blank out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review suggested blanks with explanations">
    Each suggestion comes with an explanation of why that word was selected — for example, because it's a key vocabulary term or a central fact in the passage. Read the explanations before deciding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept or dismiss each suggestion">
    Accept the suggestions that make sense for your learning objectives and dismiss any that don't fit. You're not committed to the AI's list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add answer variants and hints manually">
    For each accepted blank, add any acceptable answer variants (synonyms, spelling variants) and optionally attach a hint for students who get stuck.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the AI selects blanks

The AI doesn't blank out words at random. It targets:

- **Key concepts** — terms or ideas that are central to understanding the passage.
- **Vocabulary** — subject-specific words that students should be able to recall and spell.
- **Important facts** — specific names, dates, or values that anchor the content.

For every suggestion, the AI provides a plain-language explanation so you can evaluate its reasoning and decide whether the blank fits your assessment goals.

## Staying in control

AI suggestions are exactly that — suggestions. You make every final decision:

- **You decide which blanks go in.** Accept only the suggestions that align with what you're testing.
- **You set answer variants and hint leniency.** The AI does not configure marking rules. You define what counts as a correct answer and whether hints deduct points.
- **AI provides suggestions, not final answers.** The published activity reflects your choices, not the AI's defaults.

<Tip>
  Start with the AI's suggestions and refine from there — it's significantly faster than manually identifying blanks from a long passage, and the explanations help you think through your assessment intent.
</Tip>
