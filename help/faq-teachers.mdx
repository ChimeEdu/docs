## Getting Started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is ChimeEdu free?">
    Yes — ChimeEdu offers a **Free plan** that costs nothing and requires no credit card to sign up. The Free plan gives you one classroom and up to five permanent activities and assessments, plus unlimited temporary (Quick) activities that expire after 8 hours. It's a fully functional starting point for any teacher who wants to try interactive learning in their classroom.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's included in the Free plan?">
    The Free plan includes everything you need to get started:

    - **1 Classroom** — organize your students, activities, and study materials in one place
    - **5 permanent Activities & Assessments** — create quizzes, maps, flash cards, timelines, fill-in-the-blank exercises, and assessments that live on your dashboard indefinitely
    - **Unlimited Quick (temporary) Activities** — spin up a live quiz or activity on the fly; these expire automatically after 8 hours

    No credit card is required. You can upgrade to Plus or Premium at any time when you're ready for more.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I try Plus or Premium features?">
    Yes — you can subscribe to either plan yourself, right in ChimeEdu. Plus (\$10/month) adds Google Drive integration, Per-Student and Group issuance, and the Graphs & Charts, Diagrams, and Experiences activity types, and raises your limits to 5 classrooms and 100 activities; Premium (\$25/month) adds Study Guides (including AI-assisted drafting) and lifts your activities limit to unlimited, with 25 classrooms. You can subscribe, switch, or cancel yourself at any time; see [Billing & Subscriptions](/teachers/billing-and-subscriptions).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Classrooms

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I add students to my classroom?">
    The easiest way is to share your classroom's **join link** with your students. Open the classroom and click **Share** at the top, or use **Copy link** on the **Hand this out** card on the **Today** tab. Students without an account click the link, create a student account, and they're enrolled as soon as they finish. Students who already have an account need the classroom's **join code** instead — they enter it on their student dashboard. See [Enroll Students](/teachers/classrooms/enroll-students).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I have multiple classrooms?">
    The number of classrooms you can create depends on your plan:

    | Plan | Classrooms |
    | --- | --- |
    | Free | 1 |
    | Plus | 5 |
    | Premium | 25 |

    If you need more classrooms than your current plan allows, you can upgrade yourself at any time — see [Billing & Subscriptions](/teachers/billing-and-subscriptions).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can students see each other's work?">
    It depends on the activity type. For **live quizzes**, the leaderboard lives on the host screen — students only see it if you project or share it, and nothing is kept once the session ends. For **assessments**, each student sees only their own work: their score and results page appear once you send results, and no student can open another's. **Experiences** are written entirely by you — students read them and don't contribute entries. **Flash card** progress is personal to each signed-in student and isn't shared. Maps, timelines, and Line/Scatter charts issued **Per-Student** keep each student's work private from their classmates — see [Per-Student & Groups](/teachers/activities/issuance); in **Group Assignment** mode a group's members share one copy with each other.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Activities

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do students need an account to join a quiz?">
    No — students can join a live quiz as a **guest** using just the 6-character Quiz ID. They don't need an account. Signing in makes no difference to what's kept: ChimeEdu doesn't store quiz results for anyone, guest or signed-in. Scoring lives in the host's browser for the duration of the session and is gone once it ends, so treat a quiz as a live activity rather than an assessment. If you need a record, use an **assessment** instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does a Quick Activity last?">
    Quick Activities (temporary activities) are designed for one-off lessons and in-class sessions. They **automatically expire after 8 hours**. After that, the join code stops working and the activity is no longer accessible. If you need an activity to persist beyond a single class session, create a permanent activity from your dashboard instead — these stay on your account until you delete them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I reuse activities across classrooms?">
    Yes. Any activity — a quiz, map, timeline, flash card deck, fill-in-the-blank, graph, or diagram — can be linked to **several of your classrooms at once**. It stays one activity: edit it once and every class sees the change, and it counts once toward your plan. Each class gets its own dates, and each class's student work stays in that class. See [Use One Activity in Several Classes](/teachers/activities/several-classes). Assessments, study guides, and Experiences can be linked to several classrooms too.

    To reuse an activity **next term** with a different class, [archive it](/teachers/activities/archive-and-restore) when the unit ends and restore it later.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A unit is over — do I have to delete the activity?">
    No — **archive it instead**. Archiving removes the activity from all its classes, hides it from students (old links show an "archived" notice), and stops it counting toward your plan's activity limit, while keeping everything you built. When you teach the unit again, restore it into any classroom — student work from the previous run is cleared so it comes back fresh, and your own content is kept. Archived activities are held for 365 days. See [Archive & Restore](/teachers/activities/archive-and-restore).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Assessments

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are assessments auto-graded?">
    Partially. **Multiple choice, multi-select, true/false, numeric, matching, ordering, and image select** are scored automatically on submission. Students see **nothing** until you explicitly **send results** — marking and releasing are separate steps, so auto-scoring never reveals anything on its own. Once you send them, the student gets a notification, a prompt on their dashboard, and an email, and can open their own results page showing per-question points, any overall letter grade you set, and your comments. Only they can open it; the page is tied to their account. Your marking autosaves as you enter it.

    **Written questions (short and long answer) are scored by you.** Open a submission, award points on each written response (up to that question's value), and the total recalculates. If you send results before you've finished, the student sees "Some questions are still being scored" and no percentage. See [Assessments](/teachers/assessments#reviewing-submissions-and-sending-results).

    You'll get an on-platform notification when a student submits, if you have assessment-submission notifications switched on in your settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I set a time limit on an assessment?">
    Yes. Under **Setup › Timing** you can set an **overall time limit** for the entire assessment, and each question can carry its own **per-question time limit**. You can also turn on **extended time** for individual students who have an IEP or 504 plan, on the classroom's **Students** tab. Those students get triple time on both the per-question timers and the overall timer, and can pause an assessment that's set to one sitting. For a student using password-only access, set extended time on the access code when you create it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I change my email address?">
    To change your email address, go to **Account Settings** from the sidebar menu and look for the **Email** field under your profile details. Update the address and save your changes. You may be asked to confirm the new email address before the change takes effect — check your inbox for a confirmation link.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I reset my password?">
    If you've forgotten your password, go to [app.chimeedu.com/forgot-password](https://app.chimeedu.com/forgot-password). Enter the email address associated with your account and click **Send Reset Link**. You'll receive an email with a link to set a new password. The link is time-limited, so use it promptly. If you don't see the email, check your spam or junk folder. For more help, see the [Account & Login troubleshooting guide](/help/account-and-login).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Google Drive

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which plans include Google Drive integration?">
    Google Drive integration is included on the **Plus** and **Premium** plans, and isn't available on Free. You connect your own Google account — any Google account works, and the connection is per-teacher rather than institution-wide. Once connected you can attach files and folders to classrooms, activities, assessments, study guides, and Experiences. ChimeEdu links to your files rather than copying them and can only access the specific files you pick through the Google picker — never the rest of your Drive — so edits you make in Drive show up when the file is opened.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does ChimeEdu sync file names from Google Drive?">
    No. The file name is captured when you attach the file and doesn't change afterwards — if you rename it in Drive, ChimeEdu keeps showing the original name. The **link still works**, because ChimeEdu points at the file rather than copying it; only the label goes stale. To update the name shown, remove the attachment and re-attach it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
