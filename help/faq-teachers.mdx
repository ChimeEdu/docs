## Getting Started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is ChimeEdu free?">
    Yes — ChimeEdu offers a **Free plan** that costs nothing and requires no credit card to sign up. The Free plan gives you one classroom and up to five permanent activities and assessments, plus unlimited temporary (Quick) activities that expire after 8 hours. It's a fully functional starting point for any teacher who wants to try interactive learning in their classroom.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's included in the Free plan?">
    The Free plan includes everything you need to get started:

    - **1 Classroom** — organize your students, activities, and study materials in one place
    - **5 permanent Activities & Assessments** — create quizzes, maps, flash cards, timelines, fill-in-the-blank exercises, and assessments that live on your dashboard indefinitely
    - **Unlimited Quick (temporary) Activities** — spin up a live quiz or activity on the fly; these expire automatically after 8 hours
    - **Knowledge Base access** — full access to guides, tutorials, and help articles

    No credit card is required. You can upgrade to Plus or Premium at any time when you're ready for more.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I try Plus or Premium features?">
    Plus and Premium are **not yet purchasable** — there's no checkout or upgrade screen in the product today, and payments are still to come. Plus ($$10/month) adds Google Drive integration and raises your limits to 5 classrooms and 50 activities; Premium ($$25/month) adds AI-assisted study guide drafting and raises limits to 25 classrooms and 100 activities. If you need a higher tier during the Alpha, [get in touch](https://app.chimeedu.com/contact) — tiers are currently set by the ChimeEdu team. Keep an eye on the [changelog](https://app.chimeedu.com/changelog) for announcements.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Classrooms

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I add students to my classroom?">
    The easiest way is to share your classroom's **join link** with your students. You'll find the join link on your classroom's dashboard — click the **Share** or **Invite** button to copy it. Students can click the link, create a student account (or sign in to their existing one), and they'll be enrolled immediately. You can also share a classroom join code if your students prefer to enter it manually.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I have multiple classrooms?">
    The number of classrooms you can create depends on your plan:

    | Plan | Classrooms |
    | --- | --- |
    | Free | 1 |
    | Plus | 5 |
    | Premium | 25 |

    If you need more classrooms than your current plan allows, you'll need to upgrade. Plus and Premium plans are coming soon.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can students see each other's work?">
    It depends on the activity type. For **live quizzes**, the leaderboard lives on the host screen — students only see it if you project or share it, and nothing is kept once the session ends. For **assessments**, submissions are visible only to you; students don't see a score or a results screen. **Experiences** are written entirely by you — students read them and don't contribute entries. **Flash card** progress is personal to each signed-in student and isn't shared.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Activities

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do students need an account to join a quiz?">
    No — students can join a live quiz as a **guest** using just the 6-character Quiz ID. They don't need an account. Signing in makes no difference to what's kept: ChimeEdu doesn't store quiz results for anyone, guest or signed-in. Scoring lives in the host's browser for the duration of the session and is gone once it ends, so treat a quiz as a live activity rather than an assessment. If you need a record, use an **assessment** instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does a Quick Activity last?">
    Quick Activities (temporary activities) are designed for one-off lessons and in-class sessions. They **automatically expire after 8 hours**. After that, the join code stops working and the activity is no longer accessible. If you need an activity to persist beyond a single class session, create a permanent activity from your dashboard instead — these stay on your account until you delete them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I reuse activities across classrooms?">
    Not for activities. A quiz, map, timeline, flash card deck, or fill-in-the-blank activity belongs to **one classroom at a time** — you can reassign it to a different classroom, but it can't appear in two at once. To run the same activity with several classes, duplicate it. **Assessments, study guides, and Experiences** are different: those can be linked to multiple classrooms at the same time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Assessments

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are assessments auto-graded?">
    Partially. **Multiple choice, true/false, and image select** are scored automatically on submission. Students do **not** see the result — there's no score screen or results page on the student side, so scores are for your eyes only.

    **Written questions (short and long answer) can't currently be awarded points.** They're stored and you can read every response, but there's no scoring control for them, and they still count toward the assessment's maximum — so any assessment containing them will show a lower percentage than the student actually earned. Bear that in mind when weighting an assessment, and treat written responses as qualitative for now.

    You'll get an on-platform notification when a student submits, if you have assessment-submission notifications switched on in your settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I set a time limit on an assessment?">
    Yes. When editing an assessment's settings, you can set an **overall time limit** for the entire assessment, or a **per-question time limit** that counts down independently for each question. You can also enable **extended time** for individual students who have an IEP or 504 plan — this flag bypasses the standard time limit and applies longer countdowns for that student specifically.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I change my email address?">
    To change your email address, go to **Account Settings** from the sidebar menu and look for the **Email** field under your profile details. Update the address and save your changes. You may be asked to confirm the new email address before the change takes effect — check your inbox for a confirmation link.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I reset my password?">
    If you've forgotten your password, go to [app.chimeedu.com/forgot-password](https://app.chimeedu.com/forgot-password). Enter the email address associated with your account and click **Send Reset Link**. You'll receive an email with a link to set a new password. The link is time-limited, so use it promptly. If you don't see the email, check your spam or junk folder. For more help, see the [Account & Login troubleshooting guide](/help/account-and-login).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Google Drive

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which plans include Google Drive integration?">
    Google Drive integration is included on the **Plus** and **Premium** plans, and isn't available on Free. You connect your own Google account — any Google account works, and the connection is per-teacher rather than institution-wide. Once connected you can attach files and folders to classrooms, activities, assessments, study guides, and Experiences. ChimeEdu links to your files rather than copying them and can only access the specific files you pick through the Google picker — never the rest of your Drive — so edits you make in Drive show up when the file is opened. Plus and Premium aren't purchasable yet — tiers are set by the ChimeEdu team during the Alpha.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does ChimeEdu sync file names from Google Drive?">
    No. The file name is captured when you attach the file and doesn't change afterwards — if you rename it in Drive, ChimeEdu keeps showing the original name. The **link still works**, because ChimeEdu points at the file rather than copying it; only the label goes stale. To update the name shown, remove the attachment and re-attach it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
