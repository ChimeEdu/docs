</Note>

## What to Include in a Support Request

To help the team resolve your issue as quickly as possible, include the following in your message:

- **Your account email address** — so the team can locate your account
- **A clear description of the issue** — what you expected to happen and what actually happened
- **Steps to reproduce** — if applicable, describe the sequence of actions that led to the problem
- **Browser and device info** — for example: "Chrome 125 on Windows 11" or "Safari on iPhone 15"
- **Any error messages** — copy the exact text or attach a screenshot

The more detail you provide upfront, the faster the team can help you without needing to ask follow-up questions.

## ChimeBot — AI-Powered Support Assistant

ChimeBot is ChimeEdu's built-in AI support assistant. It's available to users on **all plans** — Free, Plus, and Premium — and can answer common questions about using the platform, navigating features, and troubleshooting issues.

You can access ChimeBot directly from your **platform dashboard** — look for the ChimeBot icon or the help menu in the navigation. ChimeBot is available any time, even outside business hours, so it's a great first stop when you have a quick question.

<Note>
  ChimeBot handles common questions well, but for complex issues, account problems, or bugs, use the [contact form](https://app.chimeedu.com/contact) or [issue report form](https://app.chimeedu.com/report-issue) to reach the human support team.
</Note>
