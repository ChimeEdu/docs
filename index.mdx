## Get Started in 3 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your free account">
    Visit [app.chimeedu.com/register](https://app.chimeedu.com/register) and sign up — no credit card required. The Free plan gives you one classroom and five activities to get going.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your first classroom">
    Create a classroom and share the join link with your students. Everything you publish — quizzes, guides, assessments — will appear in their classroom feed automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your first activity">
    Pick an activity type (Quiz, Map, Timeline, Flash Cards, Fill-in-the-Blank) and publish it to your classroom. Students can join from any browser — no app install required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  ChimeEdu is free to start. The Free plan includes 1 classroom and up to 5 activities. Upgrade to Plus or Premium for more classrooms, Google Drive integration, and AI-assisted authoring. [See pricing →](/teachers/plans-and-pricing)
</Note>
