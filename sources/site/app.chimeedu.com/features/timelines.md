## Sequence is half the story.

When students understand the order of events — not just the events themselves — they start to understand causation. Timelines give that shape to content that might otherwise feel like a disconnected list of facts, dates, and names.

ChimeEdu Timelines let you build rich, media-supported event sequences that students can explore, zoom into, and navigate on their own. Each event card can hold images, rich text descriptions, and locations — giving you the tools to turn a chronology into a genuine narrative.

### Automatic Chronological Order

Add events in any order — the timeline sorts them by date automatically, so you can build the deck without worrying about sequence.

### Rich Event Cards

Each event supports a title, date, image, description, and optional location. Students get context, not just a date and a name.

### Play Mode

Present the timeline to the class in auto-advance play mode. Events reveal in sequence — perfect for guided lessons and presentations.

### Explore & Zoom

Students can scrub through the timeline, zoom to a specific period, and tap any event for the full card — on desktop or mobile.

### Works Across Subjects

History, literature, science, biography, current events — any content with a sequence benefits from the timeline format.

### Classroom Assigned

Publish directly to a classroom. Students open the activity from their feed — no link sharing, no separate logins.

## Every subject has a timeline.

The most obvious use is history — but the format is equally powerful for tracing a character's arc through a novel, plotting the stages of a scientific discovery, or mapping the evolution of a political movement over decades.

Teachers have used ChimeEdu Timelines for everything from ancient civilisations to the life cycle of a star. If the subject has a sequence, it has a timeline.
