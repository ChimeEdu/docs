## Every question type. None of the marking.

Assessments on ChimeEdu support multiple question types in a single test: multiple choice, short answer, long-form written response, and more. Objective questions are auto-graded the moment a student submits. Written responses are flagged for your review with the full context of what the student answered.

The result is an assessment workflow where you write the questions once, students sit the test in their own time, and you open a dashboard of results rather than a pile of papers. Submission tracking shows you exactly who has completed, who's still in progress, and who hasn't started — in real time.

### Auto-Grading

Multiple choice, true/false, and matching questions are graded the moment a student submits. Results land in your dashboard instantly.

### Multiple Question Types

Mix multiple choice, short answer, long-form written response, and true/false in a single assessment — as many or as few as you need.

### Submission Tracking

See in real time who's submitted, who's in progress, and who hasn't opened the assessment yet — all from a single teacher dashboard.

### Flexible Timing

Set an overall time limit or configure per-question timers. Students see a countdown; you stay in control of the assessment pace and challenge level.

### Per-Question Analytics

After submissions close, see which questions had the lowest average scores. Identify exactly where students are struggling before the next lesson.

### Classroom Linked

Assessments publish directly to your classroom. Students see them in their feed alongside activities, Study Guides, and Experiences.

## Results the moment they click submit.

When a student finishes an Assessment, their objective answers are scored immediately — no waiting for you to log in, no batch processing overnight. The student sees their score right away, and it shows up in your submission dashboard in real time.

Written responses sit in a separate review queue, so you can annotate and release those scores separately when you're ready. Everything is tracked, timestamped, and tied to the student's classroom record.
