## Your curriculum, organised your way.

A Classroom on ChimeEdu isn't just a folder. It's the connection between you, your students, and everything you've built for them. Activities, Assessments, Study Guides, and Experiences all link directly to a classroom — which means students see the right content at the right time, without needing a separate link for every resource.

You can run as many classrooms as your subscription allows, organised however makes sense for you — by subject, by year group, by period, or by term. Each one has its own enrolment, its own activity feed, and its own submission tracking — so nothing bleeds between classes.

### Organised Activity Feed

Every activity you assign appears in the classroom feed, in order. Students open the classroom and see exactly what they need to do — no hunting for links.

### One-Step Sharing

Share the classroom join link with students once. After that, everything you publish goes straight to their feed automatically.

### Instant Search

Search across all your classroom content by title, type, or date. Find any activity, assessment, or guide in seconds — even across multiple classes.

### Cross-Activity Linking

Link activities, assessments, study guides, and experiences together inside a classroom to create a coherent learning pathway for students.

### Google Drive Integration

Connect your institution's Google Drive to a classroom. Attach lesson plans, resource files, and media directly from the folders your team already uses.

### Submission Overview

See at a glance how many students have completed each activity. Drill into per-student details when you need to follow up on progress.

## The hub for everything you teach.

When a student logs in to ChimeEdu, the first thing they see is their classroom — and from there, they can reach every activity, guide, and assessment you've set for them. No separate app. No scattered links. No "where do I find it?" messages on a Sunday night.

For you, Classrooms provide a single place to see who's engaged and who isn't — across every piece of content you've published, without switching between tools or chasing down spreadsheets.
