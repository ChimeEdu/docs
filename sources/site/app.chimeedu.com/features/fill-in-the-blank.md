## Precision testing, without the marking pile.

Cloze-style questions are one of the most reliable tools in a teacher's assessment toolkit — they test recall, comprehension, and vocabulary in a way that's hard to game. The problem has always been marking them at scale.

ChimeEdu's Fill-in-the-Blank activity type handles the marking automatically, so you can write richer, more targeted passages and spend your time acting on results rather than tallying them.

## What to expect when Fill-in-the-Blank launches

### Auto-grading

Answers are graded the moment a student submits. Results land in your gradebook instantly — no manual marking, no waiting.

### Answer Variants

Define multiple acceptable answers per blank. Handle common synonyms, spelling variants, and partial matches without losing accuracy.

### Optional Hints

Attach a hint to any blank. Students can reveal it if stuck — you control whether using a hint deducts points.

### AI-Assisted Authoring

Paste in any passage and let ChimeEdu's AI suggest which words to blank out, with explanations for why each choice tests a key concept.

### Per-blank Analytics

See exactly which blanks tripped students up. Spot the concepts that need re-teaching before the next unit begins.

### Rich Passage Formatting

Write passages with headers, images, and formatted text alongside your blanks — no need to strip your source material down to plain text.

## Your content, not AI's content.

The AI assistance in Fill-in-the-Blank will always be optional — and it'll always start from what you provide. Paste in a passage from your own resources, a textbook excerpt, or something you've written yourself, and AI helps you turn it into a structured activity.

The teacher decides which blanks matter. The teacher decides how lenient the marking is. The teacher stays the author — AI is just the editor.
