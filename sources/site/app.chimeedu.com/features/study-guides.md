## Structure that actually helps students study.

A good study guide isn't just content pasted into a document. It's content that's been shaped — organised into sections and subsections, annotated with what matters, and formatted so that a student can navigate it quickly when they need to find something at 10 p.m. the night before an exam.

ChimeEdu's Study Guide editor gives you TipTap-powered rich text with headings, bullet lists, images, and embedded media — all in a structured format you design once and your students use all term. Link a guide directly to the relevant classroom, and it stays accessible right alongside the activities and assessments it supports.

### Rich Content Editor

A full-featured TipTap editor with headings, bullet lists, images, media embeds, and inline formatting — no markdown required.

### Hierarchical Sections

Organise content into nested sections and subsections. Students can collapse and expand sections to navigate complex topics without losing their place.

### Classroom Linking

Attach a Study Guide directly to a classroom. It appears in every enrolled student's activity feed without any sharing link required.

### Google Drive Integration

Link files and folders from your institution's Google Drive directly into a guide — keeping everything current without duplicating files.

### AI-Assisted Drafting

Paste in your notes and let ChimeEdu's AI help structure and expand them into a full guide draft. You stay the author — AI does the scaffolding.

### Controlled Access

Guides are only accessible to students in the linked classroom. Visibility is teacher-controlled — you decide when a guide goes live.

## AI that serves the teacher, not the other way around.

The AI assistance in Study Guides is entirely optional and always starts from what you bring. Paste in a passage from your own notes, a curriculum document, or a textbook excerpt, and ChimeEdu's AI helps shape it into a structured, student-ready guide draft.

You decide what goes in, what stays out, and what gets changed before students see it. The AI accelerates the drafting process — but the guide is always yours.
