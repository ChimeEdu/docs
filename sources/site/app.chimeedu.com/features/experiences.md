## Field trips deserve more than a permission slip.

ChimeEdu Experiences gives teachers a structured container for any real-world learning event — whether that's a single-day museum visit or a multi-week overseas journey. Students can document their observations, reflect on what they're learning, and share their work in a safe, teacher-controlled environment.

Every Experience is linked directly to your classroom, so student submissions, map check-ins, and journal entries are all in one place — no scattered Google Forms, no chasing down responses across platforms. Teachers stay in full control of what's shared and who can see it.

### Travel & Mapping

Plot your journey on an interactive map. Students can check in at locations, and teachers can track the group's route in real time.

### Student Journal Entries

Students write rich journal entries at each stop — with support for text, images, and structured prompts set by the teacher.

### Google Drive Integration

Link itineraries, worksheets, and resource files directly from your institution's Google Drive — no uploading duplicates.

### Protected Sharing

Control exactly who can see student work. Share selectively with parents, co-teachers, or keep entries private until you review them.

### Classroom Linked

Experiences are tied to your ChimeEdu classroom — student enrolment, permissions, and submissions are all managed in one place.

### Rich Media Posts

Students can attach photos and media to their entries, creating a living record of the experience that teachers can review and celebrate.

## The teacher stays the guide, even in the field.

Set structured prompts before the trip, monitor journal submissions as they come in, and approve or hold posts before they're visible to the group. Experiences is built around the understanding that you're the one responsible for what students produce — and you should have the tools to match.

Once you're back in the classroom, use the Experience log as a jumping-off point for assessment, discussion, or a Study Guide that builds on what students documented in the field.
