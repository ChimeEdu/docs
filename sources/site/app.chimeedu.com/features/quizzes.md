## Energy in the room, data on your screen.

Live quizzes do something worksheets can't: they make the whole class participate at once, in real time. The competitive element keeps students focused, and the leaderboard gives every student — not just the fastest — a reason to stay in the game.

Every quiz is also a data collection moment. You see, question by question, who's getting it and who isn't. That means a five-minute quiz can tell you more about comprehension gaps than a marked homework set you won't read until the weekend.

### Multiplayer Gameplay

Up to the whole class competing at once, all synced to the same question at the same time — with one join code, from any device.

### Per-question Timers

Set a countdown for each question independently. Short timers for recall, longer ones for reasoning — you're in control of the pace.

### Live Leaderboard

A live scoreboard updates after every question. Students see exactly where they stand, which drives engagement right to the last round.

### Instant Results

The moment a student submits, their answer is scored. You see class-wide response data per question — no waiting, no manual tallying.

### Join by Code

Students enter a 6-character code from any browser — no account creation required. Guests can play too, so no one gets left out.

### Teacher Controls

Pause mid-quiz to discuss a tricky question. Skip ahead, replay a round, or manually advance — the pace is yours, not the timer's.

## One join code. The whole class.

When you're ready, share the code and your class is in the room — within seconds. Students on phones, tablets, or laptops can all join the same session simultaneously, without needing an account or a download.

Guest mode means a student who hasn't signed up yet never gets left behind. And once the quiz ends, signed-in students' scores are automatically recorded against their classroom for your records.
