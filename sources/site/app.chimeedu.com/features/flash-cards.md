## The science of remembering.

Spaced repetition isn't a hack — it's the most well-evidenced technique in learning science. By reviewing a card at the exact moment it's about to be forgotten, students build durable long-term memory rather than cramming for a test and losing it a week later.

ChimeEdu Flash Cards are built around this principle: the deck learns which cards a student struggles with and schedules them more frequently, while cards they know confidently are shown less often. The result is a personalised study session every time — without any extra work from you or them.

### Spaced Repetition

Cards are scheduled based on how well a student knows each one. Difficult cards come back sooner; mastered ones are shown less often.

### Multiple Study Modes

Flip mode for quick recall, type-the-answer for spelling practice, multiple choice for guided revision — students switch between modes freely.

### Progress Tracking

Students see exactly which cards they've mastered, which are in progress, and which need more work — in real time, per deck.

### Teacher-Authored Decks

You build the deck; students study it. Every card stays in your classroom context, so nothing goes off-syllabus.

### Classroom Sharing

Assign a deck directly to a classroom. Students see it in their activity list the moment you publish — no link sharing required.

### Self-Paced Study

Flash Cards are available any time, from any device. Students can study during lunch, on the bus, or the night before a test.

## Study on their terms.

Not every student learns at the same pace, and not every student can study at 9 a.m. on a Monday. Flash Cards are available around the clock, from any device with a browser — so the students who need the most practice can get it whenever it works for them.

Progress saves automatically, so a student who gets through ten cards on their phone before dinner can pick up exactly where they left off on their laptop after. No set-up, no sync required.
