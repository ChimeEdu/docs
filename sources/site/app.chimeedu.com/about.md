ABOUT US

# Built for teachers. 
Loved by students.

ChimeEdu is an interactive learning platform that makes it easy to create engaging, accessible experiences for every kind of classroom.

## Our mission

ChimeEdu is dedicated to transforming education through innovative, interactive learning tools. We believe that learning should be engaging, accessible, and adaptable to the needs of both educators and students.

Our platform gives educators powerful tools to create immersive learning experiences, while giving students the opportunity to explore, interact, and learn in dynamic ways. Whether it's exploring historical events on interactive maps, participating in real-time quizzes, or completing structured assessments — ChimeEdu makes learning come alive.

## What we offer

Every tool you need to create engaging, interactive learning experiences — in one place.

### Interactive Maps

Place pins, images, and rich content on interactive maps. Perfect for geography, history, and any subject with a location.

### Quiz Activities

Real-time quiz sessions with join codes, live leaderboards, and instant results for the whole class.

### Flash Cards

Interactive flashcard decks for spaced-repetition study, with multiple study modes and progress tracking.

### Timelines

Chronological activities with events, images, locations, and rich content. Ideal for history, literature, and sequential learning.

### Fill-in-the-Blank

Text passages with precise cloze blanks — auto-graded, with optional hints and multiple answer variants.

### Assessments

Multiple question types, auto-grading, per-question analytics, and real-time submission tracking.

### Study Guides

Rich-text reference content with hierarchical sections, Drive embeds, and optional AI-assisted drafting.

### Experiences

Field trips and collaborative journeys with travel mapping, per-location prompts, and student journal entries.

### Classrooms

Organise every activity, assessment, guide, and Experience by class group — all instantly accessible to enrolled students.

### Quick Activities

Temporary activities with no account required — perfect for demos, one-off lessons, and impromptu sessions.

## Platform information

ChimeEdu is built with modern web technologies to provide a fast, reliable, and user-friendly experience. Our platform supports multiple activity types, real-time collaboration, and comprehensive classroom management tools.

We offer flexible account tiers to meet the needs of different users, from individual educators to large educational institutions. Our free tier provides access to core features, while **Plus** and **Premium** tiers offer expanded capabilities for more intensive use.

Whether you're a teacher creating engaging lessons, a student participating in interactive activities, or an administrator managing multiple classrooms — ChimeEdu provides the tools you need for effective digital learning.
