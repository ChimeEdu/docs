## 2\. Description of Service

ChimeEdu is an interactive learning platform designed for educational purposes. The Platform provides tools and features for creating, managing, and participating in educational activities, including but not limited to:

- **Classrooms:** Virtual learning spaces where educators can organize activities and assessments
- **Map Activities:** Interactive map-based learning experiences with customizable pins, content, and multimedia
- **Quiz Activities:** Real-time and asynchronous quiz experiences with instant feedback and leaderboards
- **Flash Card Activities:** Interactive flashcard decks with multiple study modes and progress tracking
- **Timeline Activities:** Chronological event-based learning experiences with rich content and multimedia
- **Fill-in-the-Blank Activities:** Auto-graded sentence completion exercises with optional hints and answer variants
- **Assessments:** Structured evaluation tools for student assessment and grading
- **Experiences:** Field trip and experiential learning management with locations, travel logistics, and student journaling
- **Study Guides:** AI-assisted study guides with rich content, hierarchical sections, and embedded media
- **Quick Activities:** Temporary activities that can be created and used without account registration
- **Knowledge Base:** Documentation, guides, and help resources for platform users
- **Google Drive Integration:** Attach files and folders from Google Drive to classrooms, activities, and assessments (available for Plus, Premium, Administrator, and Super Administrator accounts)
- **Email Management:** Advanced email template system and batch email composition tools for administrators
