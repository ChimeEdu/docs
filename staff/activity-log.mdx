Every change made through the staff dashboard is recorded: invitations sent and accepted, roles changed, teachers edited or unlinked, campuses added or removed, library items shared, content reassigned, branding updated, SSO drafts saved. The **Activity log** lets Institution Admins and Leaders read that record.

Each entry is a plain sentence — for example, *Patricia Alvarez changed Marcus Bennett's role from Institution Admin to Leader* — with who did it and when. Changes made by the ChimeEdu team show **ChimeEdu** as the author; a person mentioned in an entry whose account has since been deleted appears as **a former user**.

## Filtering

Use the buttons at the top to show one area at a time:

- **People** — invitations, memberships, teacher edits, password resets
- **Campuses** — attached, created, edited, removed
- **Content** — library changes and reassignments
- **Settings** — institution details, branding, SSO drafts

The list shows the newest entries first; **Load older entries** pages back through history.

## Where else the log appears

- The **Dashboard** shows the six most recent entries under **Recent activity**.
- The **Reassign** page shows recent reassignments, so a move can be traced and, if needed, reversed.

<Note>
  The log is append-only. Nothing in it can be edited or deleted from the dashboard, which is what makes it useful as an audit trail.
</Note>
