The **Performance** page answers two questions for Institution Admins and Leaders: *how are our students doing* and *how much are we using ChimeEdu*. Everything on it is institution-wide. There is deliberately no per-student and no per-teacher view.

## Privacy rules built into the numbers

- Every outcome figure is an average over a group of **at least 10 students**. Smaller groups show "Fewer than 10 students" instead of a number.
- A group backed by a **single teacher** is hidden too ("Hidden to protect privacy"), because a one-teacher number would effectively be a rating of that teacher.
- When hiding one small group would let someone work it out by subtraction from the total, the next-smallest group is hidden as well.

These thresholds are fixed across ChimeEdu and can't be lowered by an institution.

## Outcome metrics

| Metric | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Assessment scores** | Average percentage on graded assessments |
| **Fill-in-the-Blank** | Average percentage on completed fill-in-the-blank activities |
| **Flashcard mastery** | Share of flash cards students have marked mastered |
| **Graph participation** | How many students have submitted a graph or chart (graphs have no score) |

Quizzes, maps, timelines, diagrams, Experiences, and study guides don't record per-student results, so they're listed under **Not tracked**.

Use the **Time window** control (all time, last 12 months, 90 days, 30 days) to narrow everything on the page.

## Breaking results down

The **By campus / grade band / subject** table shows one metric at a time across your campuses, or across the grade bands and subjects teachers have set on their classrooms.

<Tip>
  Grade band and subject come from each classroom's settings in the teacher app. If most of your results land in "Unspecified," ask teachers to set them — it's a two-field change on the classroom page.
</Tip>

**Assessment grade distribution** shows what share of graded work fell into each letter grade, again only when enough students are behind it.

## Adoption

The **Adoption** section shows how the institution is using ChimeEdu — counts, not outcomes, so the privacy thresholds above don't apply, but still never broken down by teacher:

- **Active teachers** out of your total (signed in within the time window; all-time means ever signed in)
- **Students enrolled** in your teachers' classrooms
- **Classrooms**, how many have a grade band or subject set, and how many were created in the window
- **Activities** in total and by type, plus assessments and study guides, with what's new in the window
- **Library resources** you've shared and how many times teachers have copied them

Totals are always all-time; the "new" figures follow the time window.
